Tour Godz Presents: “I’m Up Next” Concert Showcase

📅 Saturday, October 25, 2026
🕒 7:00 PM – 2:00 AM
📍 Chuck Alibi’s
7301 Oxford Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19111
🎤 Hosted by Bump Jay & Hollywood Him
🎵 Sounds by DJ Weird Nasty

Philadelphia is getting ready for a night dedicated to the city’s next generation of music talent.

Tour Godz presents “I’m Up Next” Concert Showcase, a night built to bring emerging artists, DJs, producers, fans, managers, influencers, and music supporters together under one roof for live performances, networking, and high-energy entertainment.

More than just a concert, I’m Up Next is about creating a platform for artists who are ready to take their careers to the next level. Performers get the chance to introduce their music to new audiences while connecting with people working throughout Philadelphia’s music and entertainment scene.

What to Expect

  • Live artists and DJs
  • Networking with industry contacts
  • Music, culture, and big Philadelphia energy
  • The next wave of talent taking the stage
