Federal Investigation Leads to Seven Arrests in Alleged Las Vegas Fentanyl Network

National | Crime

Published: Monday, August 10, 2026

A federal investigation into suspected drug trafficking in Las Vegas has resulted in charges against seven people accused of operating a fentanyl distribution operation and possessing firearms in connection with the alleged enterprise.

Federal prosecutors say the investigation focused on Adrian Ortiz Moreno, also known as “El Apache,” and Brenda Maria Moreno De Castro. Authorities allege the pair were involved in a continuing operation that moved at least 400 grams of fentanyl between 2024 and early August 2026.

Five additional people were named as defendants in the case: Martin Castro Jr., David Romero Hernandez, Edgar Rosas, Juan Martinez Ramirez and Walter Estrada Carlos.

Investigators moved to execute search warrants at two residences on Aug. 2 as the investigation intensified.

At one property, federal agents reported recovering approximately 12 firearms, including semi-automatic rifles, along with a significant amount of money. One of the weapons reportedly contained an engraving bearing the nickname “El Apache.”

A search of another residence produced 36 packages that authorities identified as suspected fentanyl. Investigators also recovered three semi-automatic firearms from that location.

The case followed earlier enforcement activity involving vehicle stops. During those operations, authorities said they seized roughly 40 kilograms of fentanyl and about $200,000 in cash.

Prosecutors allege the defendants were connected through a broader trafficking operation responsible for distributing large quantities of fentanyl in the Las Vegas area. The firearms recovered during the investigation have also led to weapons-related allegations.

Federal officials have not characterized the defendants as convicted criminals, and the charges remain accusations at this stage. Each defendant is presumed innocent unless prosecutors establish guilt in court.

The investigation remains part of a broader federal effort to target large-scale fentanyl distribution and suspected connections between narcotics trafficking and illegal firearms activity in Las Vegas.
