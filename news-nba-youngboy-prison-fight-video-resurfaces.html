NBA YoungBoy Prison Fight Video Resurfaces, Revealing 2021 Jail Altercation

Music | Controversy

Published: Monday, September 7, 2026

LOUISIANA — A surveillance video from 2021 involving rapper NBA YoungBoy has resurfaced online, giving the public a look at a physical confrontation that took place while the artist was being held at a Louisiana correctional facility.

The footage began making the rounds on social media on September 6, 2026, years after the incident occurred at the St. Martin Parish Correctional Center.

NBA YoungBoy, born Kentrell DeSean Gaulden, was 21 when the confrontation took place on August 3, 2021. The other inmate involved was reportedly 43.

Watch the Video

Surveillance Camera Captures Confrontation

The video begins with Gaulden inside the facility's General Holding area. He can be seen sitting at a table while apparently playing cards and watching television.

Another inmate approaches him, and a confrontation follows. The two men begin throwing punches before correctional officers rush into the area to intervene.

Officers work to pull the men apart, although the altercation briefly continues. Additional correctional staff eventually gain control of the situation, with the other inmate being taken away from the housing area.

The recording captures the fight itself, but there is no audio or footage establishing exactly what was said between the men before the confrontation began.

Report Raises Questions About Tensions Inside the Facility

The resurfaced video has also drawn attention to an investigative report attributed to the St. Martin Parish Sheriff's Office, which contains accounts from inmates regarding conditions inside the dorm.

One inmate reportedly described Gaulden as a bully and told investigators that he was afraid of the rapper.

The report also included claims concerning Gaulden's alleged behavior in the shared housing area. Among the allegations were disputes over the television remote and complaints that loud audio was being played while other inmates were trying to sleep.

Investigators also documented allegations from inmates who claimed Gaulden made comments about having the ability to harm or “touch” their family members.

Those accusations remain claims contained in the investigative report and should not be interpreted as independently confirmed facts. The surveillance footage itself does not show or establish that any of the alleged threats occurred.

The other inmate involved in the fight has not been publicly identified in the material reviewed.

Fight Happened During NBA YoungBoy's Federal Case

Gaulden was incarcerated at the time as he dealt with federal firearm charges.

His federal case was connected to a September 2020 arrest in Baton Rouge. He was later arrested in Los Angeles in March 2021 after authorities issued a federal warrant.

That placed the rapper in federal custody at the St. Martin Parish facility when the August 2021 confrontation occurred.

Several months later, on October 26, 2021, Gaulden was released from custody and placed under house arrest while his legal proceedings continued.

Old Footage Becomes New Viral Topic

More than five years after the confrontation, the previously unreleased jail footage is now generating a new wave of online discussion.

Social-media users have been analyzing the brief fight, the size difference between the two inmates and the circumstances surrounding the confrontation. The accompanying allegations from the investigative report have also become part of the online conversation.

However, the viral reaction does not provide independent confirmation of the allegations described in the report.

What can be established from the video is that Gaulden and another inmate engaged in a physical altercation and that correctional officers intervened. The events that allegedly led to the fight require separate consideration of the investigative report and inmate statements.

The resurfaced footage has effectively reopened public interest in an incident that had remained largely out of view since it occurred in 2021.
