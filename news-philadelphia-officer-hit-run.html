Philadelphia Officer Placed on Leave After Man Dies in Hit-and-Run Following Police Encounter

National | Crime

Published: Friday, August 28, 2026

A Philadelphia police officer has been placed on administrative leave after investigators determined that the officer encountered a man lying in a roadway shortly before the man was struck and fatally injured by a hit-and-run driver.

The incident happened early Thursday morning in Philadelphia’s Olney neighborhood, where a 48-year-old man was found critically injured after being hit along Rising Sun Avenue. Authorities are now investigating both the deadly crash and the officer’s actions before and after the collision.

According to Philadelphia police officials, the man was walking along the 5500 block of Rising Sun Avenue at approximately 1:40 a.m. when an officer assigned to the 35th District came across him.

Police said the man appeared to have an unsteady gait. Surveillance footage reviewed by investigators showed the officer pull alongside him and begin an interaction.

During the encounter, the man fell to the ground in the bike lane, with part of his body extending into the area used by passing traffic.

The officer reportedly got out of the patrol vehicle and spoke with the man while he remained on the ground. Investigators said the officer eventually returned to the vehicle and drove away, leaving the man where he had fallen.

The circumstances became even more serious only minutes later.

Investigators said another vehicle stopped near the man. A second vehicle then approached and maneuvered around the stopped car by entering the bike lane.

That vehicle struck the man and continued driving without stopping.

Police described the suspected hit-and-run vehicle as a green 2023-to-2026 Kia Sportage X-Line equipped with black-and-chrome rims. Investigators believe the vehicle continued eastbound onto Tabor Road after the collision.

Witnesses eventually moved the injured man out of the roadway and contacted emergency services.

What investigators later learned about the officer’s conduct after the collision has become a central part of the internal investigation.

Police said the same officer who had encountered the man before the crash later returned to the scene after emergency personnel were called. Officials allege that the officer again failed to provide assistance and did not complete a police report concerning the incident.

The Philadelphia Police Department’s Crash Investigation Division did not initially know about the collision because no report had been filed, according to officials. Investigators were alerted after the medical examiner notified police that a person had died following a crash.

Detectives then reconstructed the events using surveillance footage from nearby businesses and city cameras.

The victim was eventually taken to a hospital, where he was pronounced dead at approximately 6:15 a.m. His identity has not been publicly released as authorities work to notify his relatives.

Police department launches internal investigation

First Deputy Commissioner John Stanford addressed the incident during a Friday news conference and sharply criticized the officer’s alleged failure to assist the man.

Stanford said officers are expected to demonstrate compassion and take action when encountering someone who may be injured, impaired or experiencing a medical emergency.

He characterized the officer’s actions as deeply concerning and said the conduct did not reflect the standards expected of Philadelphia police officers.

The officer, whose name has not been released, has approximately two years of service with the department. Police have placed the officer on administrative leave while an internal investigation examines the encounter.

Authorities have not announced any disciplinary charges against the officer.

The department is also continuing its effort to identify the driver responsible for the fatal collision.

Search continues for suspected hit-and-run driver

Investigators are asking anyone who may have witnessed the crash or recognizes the described Kia Sportage to contact Philadelphia police.

The vehicle is believed to be a jungle-green Kia Sportage X-Line from model years 2023 through 2026. Police said it has distinctive black-and-chrome wheels and was last seen traveling eastbound toward Tabor Road.

The investigation remains active, with detectives reviewing surveillance footage and other evidence in an effort to determine exactly what happened in the moments before and after the collision.

The case now involves two separate investigations: one focused on finding the driver who allegedly fled after striking the pedestrian and another examining whether the responding officer failed to fulfill his responsibilities during the encounters with the victim.

For the victim’s family, the investigation comes as authorities work to answer how a man who had already been encountered by a police officer ended up remaining in the roadway before being struck by a vehicle.

Police have not yet released the victim’s identity or announced an arrest in connection with the hit-and-run.
