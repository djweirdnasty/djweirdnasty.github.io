Nolan Wells’ Final Hours Raise New Questions as Friends and Parents Give Conflicting Accounts

National

Published: Saturday, August 15, 2026

More than a month after 18-year-old Nolan Wells disappeared during a Fourth of July trip to Mississippi’s Horn Island and was later found dead, his family is demanding answers while friends are publicly explaining what they say happened before he vanished.

The mystery surrounding the death of 18-year-old Nolan Wells continues to grow, with his family demanding transparency and several of the friends who were with him on July 4 now publicly describing their final interaction with the teenager.

Wells disappeared during a Fourth of July gathering on Horn Island, a barrier island off Mississippi’s Gulf Coast. His body was discovered two days later, on July 6.

Authorities have said they do not currently suspect foul play, but Wells’ parents have repeatedly questioned the circumstances surrounding his disappearance — particularly why their son would allegedly choose to remain on the island without his phone while the rest of his group left.

Friends Describe Their Last Moments With Nolan

Several friends who were with Wells that day recently appeared on The Officer Tatum Show, where they offered their version of the events leading up to his disappearance.

Jax Pitalo, Morgan Seymour and Warren Hudson said Wells decided to stay behind after meeting a young woman at the gathering.

According to their account, the group was preparing to leave when their boat began taking on water.

Pitalo said another friend, identified as Wyatt, urged Wells to get on the boat and leave with them.

Wells allegedly refused, telling the group that he had another way to get back to the mainland.

The friends also said Wells had left his cellphone on the boat because he was trying to protect it from getting wet.

That detail has become one of the major points of concern for Wells’ parents, who have said the circumstances do not fit their understanding of their son.

The friends maintain that Wells knowingly stayed behind and believed he had another ride available.

A Troubled Boat and an Emergency Call

The friends also addressed an emergency towing call involving the boat.

Audio from the call was later released publicly and became a major source of online speculation. Some people examining the recording believe they can hear Wells’ name or other alarming statements.

The friends strongly disputed the idea that Wells was aboard the boat during the call.

They said the group was simply attempting to get the damaged vessel operating again while another boat assisted them.

Eventually, the boat was reportedly towed far enough to get back up and running.

The conflicting interpretations of the emergency call have continued to fuel speculation online, although the recording itself has not established that Wells was on the boat.

The Search Begins

According to Hudson, he eventually contacted Wells’ mother later that night after the group realized their friend was missing.

Wells’ parents have described receiving information that left them immediately concerned.

Christine Wells-Wonsley said she initially believed Nolan was still with his friends. She said she was surprised when she was told he had remained on the island with a girl he had recently met.

For his mother, the explanation didn’t make sense.

She has repeatedly emphasized that Nolan was not the type of person she believed would voluntarily isolate himself on an island without his phone.

The family also reportedly checked Life360 information and believed Nolan’s phone was back in the Ocean Springs area, adding another layer of confusion.

Family and Friends Clash During the Search

As the search intensified, tensions reportedly rose between Wells’ loved ones and some of the friends who had been with him.

The friends said members of Wells’ family and a larger group of his friends questioned them about what had happened and searched the boat they had returned on.

Pitalo said people also came to his home looking for answers.

He described retreating to his room after learning that Nolan was missing, saying he was overwhelmed by the situation.

According to the friends, another group later demanded information about where Wyatt lived and went to his home.

Police were eventually contacted after tensions escalated.

Pitalo’s brother also reportedly called law enforcement after people gathered outside the home and began pounding on the door.

The friends say the situation became increasingly intense as online theories about Nolan’s disappearance began spreading.

Nolan’s Parents Say They Felt Authorities Were Not Moving Fast Enough

While the friends have defended their actions, Nolan’s parents have questioned the response to their son’s disappearance.

Christine Wells-Wonsley said she contacted several agencies after becoming concerned, including the Mississippi Department of Marine Resources, Ocean Springs police and the Jackson County Sheriff’s Department.

The family said they encountered confusion over which agency had jurisdiction before the Jackson County Sheriff’s Department ultimately handled the case.

Wells-Wonsley recalled meeting an officer at a McDonald’s parking lot after midnight to provide information.

She said she was surprised by what she perceived as a lack of urgency.

The following morning, Wells’ family and people close to him participated in the search.

His former high school football coach reportedly helped the family search the island by boat.

Wells’ body was ultimately discovered on July 6.

Autopsy Results Remain a Major Question

More than a month later, the exact circumstances surrounding Wells’ death remain unclear to the public.

The state autopsy has not been released.

Jackson County authorities have said they do not suspect foul play, while the district attorney has indicated that the autopsy information will remain confidential until a grand jury process takes place.

Wells’ family also commissioned an independent autopsy. According to reports, that examination was unable to establish a definitive cause of death or rule out every possibility because of limitations involving the condition of the body.

The independent examination reportedly did not identify significant traumatic injuries.

Authorities have previously pointed toward the possibility that Wells drowned in an area known for strong currents and dangerous rip currents.

However, his family continues to seek additional answers.

Ben Crump, Al Sharpton Join Family’s Call for Answers

The case has now moved beyond Mississippi, with prominent civil rights figures joining Wells’ parents in their push for greater transparency.

Christine Wells-Wonsley and Elmore Wonsley recently appeared at the National Association of Black Journalists convention in Atlanta alongside civil rights attorney Ben Crump and Rev. Al Sharpton.

The family is calling for an independent federal review of the investigation.

Georgia Rep. Lucy McBath and Congressional Black Caucus Chair Yvette D. Clarke have also been among the public figures urging additional scrutiny.

Crump has emphasized that the family is seeking answers rather than social-media speculation.

The case has also sparked discussions about race after Wells, who was Black, disappeared while traveling with three white friends.

Critics online have questioned whether race affected the response to the disappearance. Those concerns remain allegations and questions, however, and there has been no public finding establishing that racial discrimination played a role in Wells’ death or the investigation.

Likewise, the online speculation surrounding Wells’ friends has not established that they were responsible for his death.

A Family Still Waiting for Answers

The case has become increasingly emotional for Wells’ family as they approach what should have been a milestone birthday.

Nolan would have turned 19 on August 19.

Instead, his parents are spending the weeks surrounding his birthday trying to understand what happened during the final hours of their son’s life.

His mother remembered Nolan as a young man whose absence has left an enormous void in the family.

For the friends who were with him on July 4, the situation has also become deeply painful. They say they loved Nolan and have faced intense harassment as theories surrounding his death have spread online.

For everyone involved, one fact remains undisputed: Nolan went to Horn Island on July 4 and never made it home.

His friends say he chose to remain behind.

His parents say that explanation raises more questions than it answers.

Authorities say there is currently no indication of foul play.

But with the state autopsy still unreleased and the family’s independent examination unable to determine a definitive cause of death, many questions remain unanswered.

What happened to Nolan Wells after his friends left Horn Island — and why did an 18-year-old who was reportedly without his phone end up dead two days later?

That is the question Nolan’s family continues to demand an answer to.
