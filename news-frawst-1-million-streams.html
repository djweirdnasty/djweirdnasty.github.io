Frawst Celebrates 1 Million Spotify Streams With “Broken”

Music | Hip-Hop

Published: Tuesday, August 18, 2026

New York independent artist Frawst has reached a major milestone with his single “Broken,” which has officially passed 1 million streams on Spotify.

The accomplishment adds another highlight to Frawst’s growing independent career. The song has also generated attention on YouTube, where the single has collected approximately 47,000 views.

An Alternative Sound With a New York Edge

Frawst has developed a style that moves between melodic hip-hop, alternative music, indie and hyperpop. His music reflects two very different environments: the energy of New York City’s Hell’s Kitchen and the more isolated atmosphere of the Pennsylvania countryside.

That contrast has become part of his artistic identity, helping shape the reflective and emotionally driven approach heard throughout his music.

From Indie Artist to Cyberpunk 2077

Frawst has already experienced an unusual career highlight for an independent rapper.

In 2020, his vocals appeared on music connected to Cyberpunk 2077 through producer Konrad OldMoney. His contributions included tracks such as “Barrio,” “Heave Ho” and “Frost,” exposing his work to an international gaming audience.

Building Without a Major Label

Frawst continues to release music independently through Soho Sounds. His catalog includes the Rebirth of Broken Dreams EP along with singles including “67” and “AM to the PM.”

The success of “Broken” shows how an independent release can steadily find an audience through streaming platforms. Passing the million-stream mark gives Frawst another strong achievement as he continues developing his sound and expanding his reach.

For an artist operating independently, 1 million Spotify streams is more than a number—it is a sign that the audience is listening.
