Bucks County Preschool Worker Charged After Alleged Assault of 2-Year-Old

National News

Published: Tuesday, August 11, 2026

A former employee at a Bucks County preschool is facing criminal charges after authorities say she allegedly assaulted a 2-year-old child in her care.

Erin Burke, 27, was arrested and charged with simple assault and endangering the welfare of a child following an investigation into an incident at Newtown Discovery Preschool in Newtown, Pennsylvania.

According to investigators, authorities were alerted to a possible child abuse case in late July after the preschool reported an incident involving Burke and one of its 2-year-old students.

The school said Burke was immediately removed from the facility after the incident was reported. The child’s parents and the appropriate authorities were also notified.

Following its own internal investigation, the preschool terminated Burke’s employment.

In a statement, Newtown Discovery Preschool described the allegations as a serious violation of the standards and responsibilities expected of employees who care for children.

The school also expressed concern for the child and family, saying the situation has affected the broader school community, including families and staff members.

Authorities have not publicly disclosed what allegedly occurred between Burke and the child. Additional details about the circumstances surrounding the alleged assault have not been released.

The charges against Burke are allegations, and she is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
