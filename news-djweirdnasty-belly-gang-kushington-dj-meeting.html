DJWEIRDNASTY Joins National DJ Meeting Featuring Belly Gang Kushington

Music | Hip-Hop

Published: Sunday, September 20, 2026

DJWEIRDNASTY recently took part in a national DJ Zoom meeting alongside fellow rapper Belly Gang Kushington, also known by his real name, Victor Thomas. The virtual gathering brought together members of the DJ Coalition, Nerve DJs and Fleet DJs for a listening session focused on new music being considered for single releases and promotion.

During the meeting, Belly Gang Kushington presented two tracks that are currently being pushed as singles: “WTF Goin” featuring 21 Savage and “No Jewelry.”

The session also gave DJWEIRDNASTY an opportunity to speak directly with Kushington and ask about his creative process.

From the Streets to the Studio

DJWEIRDNASTY asked Kushington what kind of mindset or “mode” he gets into when creating songs such as “Friend Do,” “No Jewelry” and “WTF Goin.”

Kushington explained that he doesn’t necessarily put himself into a particular creative mode before recording. Instead, he said his music often develops naturally from whatever is happening around him.

“I don’t be any mode. 95% of the time it just be whatever comes to mind.”

Kushington went on to explain that his experiences can immediately influence what he creates.

“I’m a street rapper, so I can be right on the block shooting dice and then go straight to the booth right after, and whatever happens during that range normally shapes my mindset on making a track.”

His comments offered a look into how his surroundings and everyday experiences can become part of his songwriting process.

DJs Get an Early Look at New Music

The meeting provided DJs from different organizations with an opportunity to hear the new material directly and potentially become part of the promotional push surrounding the records.

For DJWEIRDNASTY, the event was also an opportunity to connect with other DJs and artists on a national level while participating in a discussion about the music being prepared for release.

With “WTF Goin” featuring 21 Savage and “No Jewelry” among the records being presented, the listening session gave participating DJs an early introduction to Kushington’s latest material.

DJWEIRDNASTY continues to use his platform across DJing, radio, music promotion and his growing SOL DJ-booking platform to connect artists with audiences and members of the DJ community.
