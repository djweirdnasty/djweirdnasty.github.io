🎙 Murrdah Season Monday Shines Spotlight on MISLeemo, Ro-Digga and Mike Loud

This Week on MURRDAH SEASON MONDAY

This week’s episode of MURRDAH SEASON MONDAY brought together music, comedy, entertainment and trending topics as the Flavor Team welcomed several special guests.

The episode featured 16-year-old artist MISLeemo, who shared his story of selling natural toothpaste to help earn money for studio time. The young artist also joined Ayo Weird for a discussion about trending stories, including the lawsuit involving Sexyy Red and a Florida case involving the death of an infant following a reported skull injury.

Comedian Ro-Digga officially joined the Flavor Team cast as a host of Murrdah Season Monday. During the show, Ro-Digga also promoted his upcoming comedy show, “Life Is Funny.”

Content creator Mike Loud stopped by to promote the upcoming film “What Ticket,” which is scheduled to hit theaters on September 29.

Ayo Weird also used the episode to promote the upcoming BBW Bunnies and Dons Tour: Jersey Jam, taking place in Philadelphia.

One of the episode’s biggest moments came after MISLeemo’s performance. Following his set, the 16-year-old received a phone call from Philadelphia rapper Skrilla, who was recently named a 2026 XXL Freshman. The surprise call added another memorable moment to an episode focused on giving emerging talent a platform.
