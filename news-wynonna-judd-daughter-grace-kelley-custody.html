Wynonna Judd’s Daughter Grace Kelley Remains in Custody as Judge Rejects Request to Leave Jail for Birth

Music | Celebrity

Published: Friday, August 28, 2026

Grace Pauline Kelley, the daughter of country music star Wynonna Judd, was denied a temporary release from jail to give birth to her second child, according to recently surfaced court records.

Kelley, 30, is serving a 15-month sentence at Rockingham County Jail in Harrisonburg, Virginia. Her attorney sought permission for her to temporarily leave custody so she could deliver her baby in a hospital.

Court documents indicate Kelley was expecting the child around Aug. 16, 2026.

Her attorney, Robert G. Munro, filed the request July 2, asking the court to allow Kelley to be released temporarily for the delivery and remain in the hospital for several days afterward.

The request was ultimately rejected by Judge Clark A. Ritchie.

Sheriff’s office was prepared to accommodate the delivery

Kelley’s attorney argued that allowing her to temporarily leave custody would make the delivery easier and permit the baby’s father and other relatives to be present.

The filing also raised concerns about the logistical burden of having sheriff’s deputies maintain custody of Kelley inside a hospital.

According to the documents, a captain with the Rockingham County Sheriff’s Office supported allowing Kelley to receive a temporary furlough.

The judge nevertheless declined to approve the request.

Instead, court records indicate that sheriff’s deputies are expected to escort Kelley to a hospital for a scheduled cesarean section.

It remains unclear whether the birth or procedure has already occurred.

Kelley is already a mother

The pregnancy marks Kelley’s second child.

She previously gave birth to her daughter, Kaliyah Grace, in 2022 while serving time in connection with a parole violation. Kelley was permitted to leave custody for the birth.

Kaliyah has since been living with her grandmother, Wynonna Judd, on the singer’s Tennessee property.

Court documents recently cited in reports state that Kelley has little or no contact with her biological family in Tennessee, including her mother.

Judd has generally kept details of her relationship with her granddaughter private, although she offered a glimpse into their home life during a January 2025 appearance on the Today show.

At the time, Judd explained that caring for her young granddaughter had changed her daily routine. She described life on her farm as quiet and centered around family, food and spending time together.

Jail sentence followed criminal case

Kelley is currently incarcerated following a guilty plea in a case involving driving with a suspended or revoked license.

Her legal troubles have included multiple arrests and convictions over the years.

Online court information cited in the reports indicates she is expected to leave custody in November 2026 and then remain under supervised probation for five years.

Her case has attracted considerable public attention because of her connection to Judd, but much of the recent focus has centered on her pregnancy and the circumstances surrounding the court’s decision to keep her incarcerated through the delivery.

Uncertainty remains over baby’s arrival

Although Kelley was reportedly due around Aug. 16, authorities and representatives have not publicly confirmed whether she has given birth.

The court’s decision means that, rather than receiving a temporary release, Kelley is expected to remain under correctional supervision while receiving medical care for the delivery.

The circumstances surrounding the birth, including whether family members will be able to visit her and the newborn, have not been fully disclosed.
