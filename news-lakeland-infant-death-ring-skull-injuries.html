Lakeland Infant Death Leads to Arrest After Investigators Link Mother’s Ring to Skull Injuries

National | Crime

Published: September 13, 2026

LAKELAND, Fla. — What initially appeared to investigators to be an accidental death involving a sleeping infant has developed into a criminal case after forensic evidence pointed to repeated trauma to the child’s head.

Jessica Vlasac, 42, was arrested in connection with the death of her 3-month-old son and charged with aggravated child abuse, according to the Polk County Sheriff’s Office.

The investigation began September 6 when emergency crews were summoned to a Lakeland home after the infant was found unresponsive. Medical personnel transported the child to a local hospital, but doctors were unable to save him.

Authorities have not publicly identified the infant.

Medical Examination Changes Direction of Case

Investigators said Vlasac initially described the baby’s death as an accident. She reportedly told deputies that she had fallen asleep with her son and later discovered him face down and unresponsive.

However, the medical examination raised questions about that account.

According to investigators, the examination revealed several separate injuries to the infant’s head. One of the wounds reportedly had a distinctive shape that medical officials believed could have been produced by a hard, square-edged object.

That finding prompted detectives to take a closer look at what may have happened inside the home.

Jewelry Becomes Key Piece of Evidence

During a subsequent interview, detectives noticed a large ring Vlasac was wearing. Investigators said the design of the ring’s stone resembled the unusual shape documented in the medical examination.

The ring was collected as part of the investigation.

Authorities said Vlasac later asked investigators whether the piece of jewelry could have caused the injuries suffered by her son. Investigators said the medical examiner ultimately connected the ring to the marks found on the child’s head.

Investigators Also Review Phone Records

The investigation expanded beyond the physical evidence.

Detectives examined messages sent around the time of the infant’s death. Authorities said those communications indicated Vlasac had consumed or purchased alcohol earlier that day and later made statements concerning the baby’s death.

Investigators also said some messages had been deleted and that the child’s father was asked to remove portions of their conversation.

The father reportedly told authorities that Vlasac’s behavior could become aggressive when she drank.

Mother Remains in Custody

Vlasac was arrested September 10 and booked into the Polk County Jail. Authorities said she remained jailed without bond while awaiting further court proceedings.

A detention hearing is scheduled for later this month, with an arraignment set for October 13.

Officials have not released the infant’s identity or provided details about whether prosecutors could pursue additional charges as the investigation continues.

For now, Vlasac faces an aggravated child abuse charge. The allegations have not been proven in court, and she is presumed innocent unless found guilty.
