The Impact Tour 2026 — New Castle, Delaware

Sunday, October 11, 2026 — Emmaculate, 3800 N Dupont, New Castle, DE

🎤🔥 MUSIC OVER VIOLENCE — THE MOVEMENT HAS BEGUN! 🔥🎤

Tourgodz + Killa Management kicked off The Impact Tour 2026 with its first stop at Emmaculate in New Castle, Delaware. D4M $loan headlined the night with a special appearance by Trap Beckham — five cities, five shows, one movement.

🎧 The building was packed wall to wall with real music, real people, and bigger opportunities. Check out 15 photos and 14 videos from the show below.
