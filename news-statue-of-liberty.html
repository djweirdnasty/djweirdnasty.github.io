Boat Captain Arrested After Fatal Capsizing Near Statue of Liberty

National News | New York

Published: Sunday, August 9, 2026

A boat captain has been arrested after a vessel overturned in New York Harbor near the Statue of Liberty, killing a 27-year-old woman and her 5-month-old daughter, authorities said.

Officials identified the captain as 46-year-old Manuel Hernandez. He was initially charged with 13 counts of reckless endangerment following Saturday night's incident. Hernandez was later placed in federal custody as the investigation was transferred to the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York.

Authorities have not yet confirmed whether Hernandez has legal representation. Court records were also not immediately available.

The incident happened at approximately 10:25 p.m. Saturday near Liberty Island. According to police, a 22-foot Bayliner pleasure boat overturned while carrying passengers in the harbor.

Thirteen people were reportedly aboard the vessel. Twelve survivors were rescued and transported to nearby hospitals after suffering minor injuries, according to the NYPD.

Among those pulled from the water were Queens resident Sara Sanchez, 27, and her infant daughter, Antonella Garcia. Police divers recovered the mother and child from the harbor. Both were taken to NYU Langone Health in Brooklyn in critical condition, but doctors later pronounced them dead.

The NYPD's emergency response included its Harbor Unit scuba team and a police helicopter.

The U.S. Coast Guard is working alongside New York authorities to determine what caused the boat to overturn and whether the vessel was being operated as an unauthorized charter.

The Coast Guard said it is conducting an active marine casualty investigation with the Coast Guard Investigative Service and NYPD. Investigators are also examining the circumstances surrounding the boat's operation, including whether it was legally permitted to carry passengers for hire.

The vessel was later towed from the harbor and remains in police custody as investigators examine the evidence.

Bayliner manufactures several boats in the 22-foot range. Depending on the specific model, the vessels can accommodate approximately 10 to 12 passengers.

Friends and neighbors remembered Sanchez as a kind and friendly woman. Diego Naranjo, 35, said he had known her for several months and described her as someone who was always respectful and pleasant.

Naranjo said Sanchez lived in the College Point section of Queens with the father of her children. He recalled seeing the family bringing groceries home just days before the tragedy.

The investigation remains ongoing, and authorities have not released additional details about what led to the vessel capsizing.

Liberty Island, located in New York Harbor, is home to the Statue of Liberty, one of the country's most recognizable landmarks. The monument was a gift from France and was formally dedicated in 1886. The island attracts visitors throughout the year, including tourists who arrive by ferry and sightseeing boats to view the statue and surrounding harbor.
