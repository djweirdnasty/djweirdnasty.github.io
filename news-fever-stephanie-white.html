Fever Rally Around Stephanie White in Statement Win Over Liberty

Sports News

Published: Tuesday, August 12, 2026

The Indiana Fever answered a week of controversy with one of their most impressive performances of the season, defeating the New York Liberty 106-92 while showing public support for head coach Stephanie White.

White addressed reporters before Tuesday's game after facing criticism over her handling of a hard foul against Fever guard Sophie Cunningham during Indiana's previous game against Chicago.

White explained that she had not seen the play when it happened or before speaking to the media afterward. After reviewing the incident, she acknowledged that the foul was more serious than she initially realized.

The league ultimately upgraded the foul committed by Chicago's DiJonai Carrington to a flagrant 2 and ejected her from the game.

White's initial comments sparked criticism from some Fever supporters, with some questioning whether she had sufficiently defended Cunningham. The controversy escalated when Indiana's lieutenant governor publicly called for White to lose her job. White was then booed by some fans during pregame introductions Tuesday.

Rather than allow the situation to fracture the team, White made it clear that she stands behind her players. She rejected claims that she does not support them and emphasized that their safety and well-being are priorities.

Cunningham responded by defending her coach and saying there is no issue between the two. She described their relationship as strong and said the criticism coming from outside the organization was simply noise that the team was refusing to let affect them.

Clark also came to White's defense. The Fever star said she was disappointed when fans booed the coach and made it clear that the players intended to stand with her.

Indiana then took that support onto the court.

The Fever controlled the game against the defending champion Liberty and built a lead of as many as 21 points. The victory moved Indiana to 21-12 and gave the team its ninth win in its last 12 games.

The Fever's offense was particularly impressive. They scored at least 100 points for the 15th time this season, setting a new WNBA single season record. Indiana also shot 16-for-25 from three-point range.

Kelsey Mitchell contributed 25 points, while Clark recorded 20 points and 10 assists. It was Clark's ninth 20-point, 10-assist game of the season, establishing another league record for a single season.

Afterward, Cunningham said the team finally felt free to enjoy itself on the court despite everything happening around the organization.

For White, the night provided a strong response to the criticism. Instead of allowing the controversy to split the Fever, the players rallied behind their coach and delivered a convincing win against one of the WNBA's top teams.
