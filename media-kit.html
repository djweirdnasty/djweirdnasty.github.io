Media Kit & Sponsorship Opportunities

DJWEIRDNASTY is a Philadelphia-born DJ, music curator, and digital media brand covering mixtapes, live events, news, music, entertainment, sports, and culture. We connect brands to a loyal, engaged audience through the website, events, social media, and original content.

Audience Overview

  • Monthly website views: 1,900+ and growing
  • Top traffic sources: Organic Social (41.8%), Direct (25.6%), Organic Search (growing)
  • Top markets: Philadelphia, New York, plus national/international reach
  • Audience interests: music, DJ culture, events, news, entertainment, sports, streetwear
  • Age demo: 18–45, event and lifestyle-focused

What We Offer Brands

  • Website banner ads — homepage, category pages, and article footers
  • Sponsored articles — featured stories and product spotlights
  • Event sponsorship — logo placement, stage banners, vendor booths, branded drink specials
  • Mixtape & playlist integration — sponsored drops, intro/outro mentions, cover art placement
  • Social media promotion — Instagram and Facebook story posts, feed posts, reels
  • Newsletter features — dedicated or included placements
  • SOL booking platform — sponsor live DJ bookings and event experiences

Sponsorship Packages

Every partnership is customizable. Typical entry points:

  • Starter ($150–$300): Website banner for 30 days + 1 social post
  • Growth ($500–$1,000): Banner + sponsored article + 3 social posts + newsletter mention
  • Event Partner ($1,000+): Event branding, stage shoutouts, drink/pour sponsorship, social coverage
  • Liquor & Lifestyle ($750+): Event activation, branded content, 21+ audience targeting, responsible messaging included

Why Partner With DJWEIRDNASTY?

  • Established local presence in Philadelphia and the surrounding region
  • Active event programming (MUD MUSIC, Murrdah Season Monday, artist showcases, SOL bookings)
  • Original content across music, news, sports, and entertainment
