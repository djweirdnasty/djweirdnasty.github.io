Berlin High-Rise Fire Turns Into Dramatic Midair Rescue

National News

Published: Friday, August 14, 2026

A woman trapped above a raging apartment fire made a terrifying escape after firefighters positioned a rescue basket beneath her.

A routine morning in Berlin’s Friedrichshain district turned into a major emergency Friday when a fire broke out inside a high-rise apartment building.

The woman became trapped on the 11th floor as flames spread through an apartment. With smoke filling the area and fire burning dangerously close to her, she made her way outside and held onto a narrow section of the building while crews worked to reach her.

Video of the incident shows firefighters approaching from below using an aerial rescue platform. However, the equipment could not initially get close enough to the woman.

With limited options remaining, she eventually released her grip and dropped toward the rescue basket.

A firefighter positioned inside the basket managed to catch her, and she was then brought safely back down to the ground.

Authorities said the woman survived and sustained minor injuries. She was taken to a hospital for additional treatment.

Dozens Forced From Their Homes

The fire triggered a significant emergency response, with residents evacuated as firefighters worked to contain the flames.

Authorities said the incident affected approximately 115 people. Multiple residents were brought out using a combination of stairwell evacuation equipment and aerial rescue operations.

Officials reported that 13 people suffered minor injuries and received medical care.

The response involved 18 fire engines, two aerial ladder vehicles and nine ambulances, along with additional emergency personnel.

Firefighters were able to bring the blaze under control within roughly three hours.

Investigation Underway

The fire reportedly began inside an apartment on the 11th floor before spreading to the unit above it. The cause has not yet been determined.

While the building suffered a serious fire, the emergency response prevented a much worse outcome.

The most dramatic moment came when the trapped woman took a chance on the rescue basket below her. The firefighter waiting underneath successfully caught her, turning a potentially fatal fall into a successful rescue.

No deaths were reported in connection with the blaze.
