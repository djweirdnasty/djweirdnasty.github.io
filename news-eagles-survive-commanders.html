Eagles Survive Commanders, 24–22, in Thrilling Week 1 Opener

Published: Sunday, September 13, 2026

The Philadelphia Eagles opened the 2026 regular season with a dramatic 24–22 victory over the Washington Commanders on Sunday at Lincoln Financial Field. The Eagles improved to 1–0, while Washington dropped to 0–1.

Jalen Hurts led Philadelphia’s offense with 203 passing yards and three touchdowns without an interception. Dallas Goedert was his biggest scoring target, catching four passes for 77 yards and two touchdowns, including a 43-yard touchdown that gave Philadelphia crucial breathing room in the fourth quarter.

New Eagles receiver Dontayvion Wicks also made an immediate impact in his Philadelphia debut, catching a 64-yard pass and adding a 9-yard touchdown.

Washington refused to go away. Jayden Daniels finished with 164 passing yards and two touchdowns, connecting with Stefon Diggs and Antonio Williams for scores. The Commanders’ final touchdown came with just over a minute remaining, cutting Philadelphia’s advantage to two points.

The game ultimately came down to one play.

Washington went for a two-point conversion with 1:01 remaining, but Philadelphia’s defense stopped the attempt. The Commanders then attempted an onside kick, but the Eagles recovered and were able to run out the clock.

Philadelphia wasn’t dominant statistically—the Eagles were out-possessed and Washington ran more offensive plays—but the Eagles made the explosive plays they needed. Saquon Barkley also contributed 83 rushing yards, including a 42-yard run.

The victory gives Philadelphia an important NFC East win to begin the season and sends the Eagles into Week 2 at 1–0.
