Google Bets Big on AI as New Pixel 11 Phones Bring Bigger Storage and Camera Upgrades

National | Tech

Published: Wednesday, August 13, 2026

Google is making artificial intelligence a bigger part of the smartphone experience with the launch of its new Pixel 11 family, while also introducing camera, storage and charging improvements across the lineup.

The company announced the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL on August 12, alongside the latest Pixel Pro Fold. Google is promoting the devices around the idea of using AI to handle everyday tasks with less interaction from the user.

Camera Upgrades

Camera design has also received attention this year. The new phones feature slimmer camera hardware, while Google says the system delivers stronger zoom performance and improved results in challenging lighting conditions.

A new photography feature called Magic Capture is designed to make capturing moments easier. With one press of the shutter, the phone can automatically record multiple photos and video instead of requiring the user to repeatedly tap the screen.

Google is also improving nighttime photography on the Pro models. Its updated Instant Night Sight system is designed to shorten the time needed to capture images in dark environments, meaning users should not have to hold the phone completely still for as long.

AI-Powered Software

The company is putting just as much emphasis on software.

Among the new AI-powered additions are live translation for videos, podcasts and voice messages. Google is also expanding speech-to-text capabilities and adding tools that can pull still images from video.

Video creators get additional features as well, including improved stabilization and a built-in teleprompter.

New Features

The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL also introduce HiLight. The feature allows users to give certain contacts a personalized color. If the phone is placed face down when one of those contacts calls, the device can illuminate using that assigned color.

Google is also expanding storage across the lineup. The new phones start at 256GB, meaning the company is dropping the 128GB configuration that previously served as the entry-level option.

Battery performance is advertised at up to 30 hours, while wireless charging is also faster than before.

Pricing

The upgrades come with higher prices. The Pixel 11 starts at $900, followed by the Pixel 11 Pro at $1,100 and the Pixel 11 Pro XL at $1,300. The Pixel Pro Fold begins at $1,900.

Those prices represent a $100 increase compared with last year’s corresponding models.

Google is attempting to make the higher-priced Pro phones more attractive by including a six-month trial of its AI Pro subscription with purchases of the Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL.

AI Strategy and Competition

The offer is part of Google’s broader effort to get more consumers using its Gemini-powered AI services as competition with companies such as OpenAI continues to grow.

Google’s strategy also comes as Apple continues expanding its own artificial intelligence offerings. While Apple has emphasized privacy and practical everyday applications, Google has made AI one of the defining features of its Pixel products.

More Hardware Announcements

The smartphone launch wasn’t Google’s only hardware announcement.

The company also introduced Pixel Tag, a compact tracking device designed for Android users. It works with phones running Android 9 or later and will cost $30 for a single unit or $100 for a four-pack.

The Bottom Line

With the Pixel 11 series, Google is betting that a combination of AI tools, upgraded photography, additional storage and improved battery performance will give consumers enough reasons to move to the newest generation.

The bigger question is whether those improvements will be enough to convince Pixel owners — and users of competing smartphones — to pay the higher prices.
