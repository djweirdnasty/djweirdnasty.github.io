Las Vegas Domestic Disturbance Turns Deadly, Leaving Officer Injured and Two Dead

National | Crime

Published: Monday, August 10, 2026

A late-night domestic disturbance in southwest Las Vegas turned into a deadly confrontation Monday morning, leaving a police officer injured and a juvenile girl and suspected gunman dead.

The incident unfolded shortly after midnight at a residence in the 4500 block of Pigeon Point Court, near South Buffalo Drive and Peace Way, according to the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department.

Police were initially contacted after a woman reported an emergency inside the home. She told a 911 dispatcher that a man was pursuing her while carrying a blowtorch and allegedly trying to set her on fire. The woman also reported that she had been shot.

When officers reached the residence, they went inside and encountered the suspected gunman behind a closed interior door. Authorities said the man then fired at the officers through the door.

One officer was struck during the exchange. Another officer fired back before police pulled away from the residence and established a perimeter, treating the situation as a barricaded suspect incident.

The wounded officer was transported to a local hospital. Police said she was in stable condition and her injuries were not considered life-threatening.

SWAT personnel later entered the residence and discovered the suspected shooter and a juvenile girl with gunshot wounds. Both were pronounced dead at the scene.

Authorities have not publicly identified the suspect or released the girl's age. Investigators also have not disclosed the circumstances that led to the girl's death.

The woman who contacted emergency services managed to escape the home. Police said she sustained minor injuries and was provided medical care.

Detectives with the department's homicide unit are investigating the shooting. The area surrounding the residence was expected to remain restricted as investigators continued processing evidence.

Monday's incident represents the 10th officer-involved shooting recorded within the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department's jurisdiction this year. It follows another deadly shooting last week that resulted in the death of a Las Vegas police officer.

Officials said the investigation remains ongoing and additional information may be released as detectives continue working to determine exactly what happened inside the residence.
