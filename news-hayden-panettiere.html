Hayden Panettiere Dies at 36: ‘Heroes’ and ‘Nashville’ Star Remembered

Entertainment News

Published: Sunday, August 16, 2026

Actress Hayden Panettiere, who became a household name through roles in Heroes, Nashville and the Scream franchise, has died at 36.

Her representative confirmed her death Sunday, August 16. Her father, Skip Panettiere, shared the family’s grief and asked for privacy as relatives process the loss.

No cause or circumstances surrounding Panettiere’s death have been publicly released.

From Child Star to Hollywood Favorite

Panettiere began performing when she was very young, appearing in commercials and television productions before establishing herself as a recognizable Hollywood talent.

Her major breakthrough came with NBC’s Heroes, where she portrayed Claire Bennet, a teenager with extraordinary regenerative abilities. The role turned Panettiere into one of the most recognizable young actresses on television.

She later starred in Nashville as Juliette Barnes, a gifted country performer whose personal struggles became a major part of the series. The role allowed Panettiere to combine acting and singing.

Her film résumé included Remember the Titans, Ice Princess, Raising Helen, I Love You, Beth Cooper and Scream 4. More than a decade after first playing Kirby Reed, she returned to the character for Scream VI in 2023.

Panettiere also voiced Princess Dot in Disney and Pixar’s A Bug’s Life and earned recognition as one of the youngest performers to receive a Grammy nomination.

She Was Open About Her Personal Battles

As her career developed, Panettiere became increasingly willing to discuss difficult experiences away from Hollywood.

Her memoir, This Is Me: A Reckoning, explored her childhood in the entertainment industry, addiction, postpartum depression, recovery and domestic abuse.

She previously revealed that her struggles with substance misuse affected her relationship with her daughter, Kaya, whom she shares with former fiancé Wladimir Klitschko.

Panettiere had spoken publicly about Klitschko taking custody of Kaya while she worked through her struggles, describing the decision as one of the most painful moments of her life.

The Loss of Her Brother

Panettiere endured another devastating loss when her younger brother, actor Jansen Panettiere, died in 2023 at just 28.

The siblings had a particularly close relationship, and Hayden later discussed how difficult it was to cope with his death.

His passing remained one of the most painful chapters of her life.

A Voice for Causes She Believed In

Panettiere wasn’t known solely for her entertainment career.

She used her platform to advocate for animal welfare, environmental causes and mental health awareness. She also became an outspoken supporter of Ukraine following Russia’s invasion.

Her connection to Ukraine was particularly personal because of her relationship with Klitschko and their daughter. She participated in humanitarian efforts aimed at helping people affected by the war.

Her advocacy was also recognized in Washington, D.C., where officials proclaimed September 16, 2011, as Hayden Panettiere Day.

Co-Stars Remember Hayden

News of Panettiere’s death quickly prompted tributes from friends and former co-stars.

David Arquette, who worked with her on Scream 4, remembered Panettiere with an emotional social media message.

Melissa Barrera, her Scream VI co-star, also paid tribute, calling her “sweet Hayden.”

Bethany Joy Lenz shared a lengthy remembrance recalling Panettiere as a remarkably intelligent and energetic child performer. She reflected on knowing Panettiere early in her career and expressed disbelief over her death.

Podcast host Jay Shetty, who interviewed Panettiere earlier this year, also shared his condolences, saying he was deeply affected by their recent conversation.

Remembering Hayden Panettiere

Panettiere spent virtually her entire life in the entertainment industry, growing from a child performer into a television star and eventually returning to the spotlight after periods away from acting.

Her performances as Claire Bennet, Juliette Barnes and Kirby Reed left lasting impressions on different generations of fans.

While her career was marked by major successes, Panettiere also used her public platform to discuss struggles that many people experience privately.

She was 36.

Her family has requested privacy, and authorities have not publicly disclosed the circumstances or cause of her death.
