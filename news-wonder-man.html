Disney+ Cancels Marvel Series 'Wonder Man' After One Season

Entertainment News

Published: Monday, August 10, 2026

Marvel fans will not be getting another season of Wonder Man after Disney+ reversed an earlier decision to continue the series.

The Marvel Studios production has been canceled despite previously receiving a Season 2 renewal. The decision ends the live-action series after its eight-episode debut season.

Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, who portrayed struggling actor and emerging superhero Simon Williams, addressed the cancellation on social media after the news became public.

Abdul-Mateen thanked viewers who supported the series and said he was proud of what the production accomplished.

"The show worked. And that's my favorite thing about it," the actor wrote, reflecting on the reaction from fans and online viewers. He also expressed appreciation for people who told him the series had encouraged them or made an impact on their lives.

Abdul-Mateen's performance earned him an Emmy nomination for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series, making the recognition notable among live-action Marvel television projects.

The first season arrived on Disney+ in January, with all eight episodes released on Jan. 27. According to Nielsen figures cited in the original report, the series accumulated approximately 618 million minutes of viewing during its opening week.

That performance placed Wonder Man eighth among streaming original programs for the Jan. 26-Feb. 1 measurement period. The audience also appeared to skew older, with viewers between 35 and 49 accounting for 41% of its total viewing during that week.

Marvel had announced the second-season renewal in March. Abdul-Mateen II was expected to return as Simon Williams, while Ben Kingsley was set to reprise his role as Trevor Slattery.

Although the series itself will not continue, the cancellation does not necessarily mean the characters have disappeared from the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Marvel Studios could potentially bring Simon Williams, Trevor Slattery or other characters connected to the show into future MCU projects.

The character of Wonder Man has a long history in Marvel Comics. Simon Williams first appeared in The Avengers #9 in 1964 and was created by Stan Lee, Don Heck and Jack Kirby.

The Disney+ adaptation approached the character from a Hollywood-focused perspective. The story followed Simon as he attempted to establish himself as an actor while struggling to land a major opportunity.

His path crossed with Trevor Slattery, the former actor portrayed by Kingsley, as the two became connected to a new superhero movie being directed by Von Kovak, played by Zlatko Burić.

The series used the fictional production of a superhero movie to explore the entertainment business while incorporating Marvel's larger superhero universe.

Despite the show's cancellation, Abdul-Mateen's Emmy recognition gives the series a notable place in Marvel's television history.

For viewers who connected with the series, however, the story ends after just one season, leaving the future of Simon Williams on screen uncertain.
