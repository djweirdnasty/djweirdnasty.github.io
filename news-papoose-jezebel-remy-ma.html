Papoose Fires Back at Remy Ma With New Diss Track “Jezebel”

Music | Entertainment

Published: September 11, 2026

The ongoing public fallout between Papoose and Remy Ma has moved back into music, with Papoose releasing a new diss track aimed at his estranged wife.

Titled “Jezebel,” the track was released September 11 along with a music video. Papoose uses the song to address several issues surrounding the couple’s highly publicized breakup, including accusations of infidelity, their disagreements over their marriage and long-running claims about who contributed to Remy Ma’s music.

In the song, Papoose makes allegations that Remy was unfaithful during their relationship and disputes her previous accusations that he cheated. He also references the support he says he provided during Remy’s time in prison, including staying in contact with her, bringing her food and helping with her bail.

Another major point of the track involves songwriting. Papoose claims he wrote some of Remy’s rhymes during their relationship, a claim Remy has previously rejected. Their conflicting accounts have become another part of their increasingly public separation.

The situation has also involved professional boxer Claressa Shields, who is currently in a relationship with Papoose. Remy and Shields have exchanged public comments as tensions surrounding the breakup have continued to play out on social media.

Papoose filed for divorce from Remy Ma in May 2025. The couple, who married in 2008 and later held a televised wedding ceremony in 2016, share a daughter.

As the divorce moves forward, both artists have continued to share their perspectives publicly, with their personal disagreements now becoming part of their music and social-media exchanges. Whether Remy responds with another song remains to be seen, but “Jezebel” has added another chapter to one of hip-hop’s most closely watched celebrity breakups.
