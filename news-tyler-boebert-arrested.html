Tyler Boebert Faces New Charges in Colorado Investigation

National | Politics

Published: Tuesday, August 11, 2026

Tyler Boebert, the 21-year-old son of U.S. Rep. Lauren Boebert, has been arrested in Colorado over allegations involving a sexually explicit recording made in 2024.

The Garfield County Sheriff’s Office said Boebert was arrested Sunday. He was later released after posting a $10,000 bond.

The case centers on an alleged video involving Boebert and a 17-year-old. Boebert was 18 when the alleged recording was made, according to an affidavit used to obtain an arrest warrant.

According to investigators, the teenager’s mother contacted authorities after discovering that a video involving her daughter had appeared on Instagram. The affidavit alleges that the recording showed the two engaging in a sexual act. Investigators have not established who allegedly posted the video to the social media platform.

Court records show Boebert has been charged with three counts of sexual exploitation of a child. He also faces two counts related to allegedly contributing to the delinquency of a minor.

Boebert has not publicly commented on the allegations. Authorities released him on bond, and it was not immediately known whether he had secured legal representation in the latest case. An attorney who represented him during an earlier legal matter did not immediately respond to inquiries.

The arrest adds to a series of legal troubles involving Boebert. He previously faced charges following incidents that police characterized as vehicle trespassing and property theft. That earlier case remains pending.

Lauren Boebert, a Republican member of Congress from Colorado, issued a statement following her son’s latest arrest.

She said she loves her son and indicated that the allegations are being taken seriously. She also expressed concern for everyone affected by the situation.

The congresswoman previously discussed her son’s difficulties after his earlier arrest, saying he had experienced significant challenges in the public eye. She said at the time that she intended to continue supporting him while encouraging him to turn his life around.

The current case involves allegations that have not been proven in court. Tyler Boebert remains legally presumed innocent unless prosecutors establish his guilt.
