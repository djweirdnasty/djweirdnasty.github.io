Sexyy Red Faces Potential $5 Million Default Judgment in Lawsuit

Music | Legal

Published: September 10, 2026

St. Louis rapper Sexyy Red is facing a potentially costly legal battle involving the grandmother of one of her children.

Nicole Barnes, 51, has asked a Missouri court to enter a $5 million default judgment against the rapper. Barnes alleges that Sexyy Red shared her home address online and encouraged her followers to confront her at her residence.

According to court documents cited in reports, Barnes claims the rapper’s online activity included telling followers to “kick the door in” at the Missouri home. Barnes says she became concerned for her safety and eventually left the residence.

Barnes first filed the lawsuit in March 2025. The latest development centers on her claim that Sexyy Red did not respond to the lawsuit by a court-imposed deadline. Barnes subsequently sought a default judgment, which could allow the court to rule in her favor if the legal requirements are met.

However, the $5 million award has not been granted. The judge still has to review Barnes’ request and determine whether a default judgment is appropriate.

Sexyy Red’s attorney, Jason Lampert, has denied the allegations. In a statement reported by TMZ, Lampert characterized the claims as without merit and said the rapper intends to defend herself in court.

The lawsuit reportedly stems from tensions involving Sexyy Red, her child’s father and Barnes, who is the child’s paternal grandmother. Barnes’ attorney previously argued that his client became involved in a dispute that he said did not concern her.

For now, the case remains unresolved. While Barnes is seeking $5 million, that amount is only a requested judgment and does not mean the rapper has been ordered to pay the money.

The court’s decision on the default-judgment request will determine what happens next in the case.
