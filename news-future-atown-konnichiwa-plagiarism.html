Future Faces Plagiarism Claims Over “Konnichiwa” From Philadelphia’s A-Town

Music | Controversy

Published: Sunday, September 6, 2026

Future is facing accusations of copying an internet personality and comedian from Philadelphia after fans noticed similarities between the rapper's “Konnichiwa” and an earlier track released by A-Town.

The controversy surfaced following the release of Future's 10th studio album, The Real Me, in July 2026. Among the album's tracks is “Konnichiwa,” a song that quickly caught the attention of listeners familiar with A-Town's music.

A-Town, a Philadelphia-based internet personality and comedian, released his own song titled “Konnichiwa” on July 5, 2024, giving his version a two-year head start over Future's track.

Social-media users began questioning whether Future's record borrowed elements from A-Town's earlier work.

Fans Point to More Than the Title

The biggest talking point is the fact that both songs share the exact same title: “Konnichiwa,” a Japanese greeting that translates roughly to “hello.”

But online discussions have gone beyond the name.

Listeners have pointed to similarities in the songs' overall concepts, Japanese-inspired themes and trap-oriented atmosphere. Some fans have also compared the accompanying visuals, arguing that Future's music video contains imagery and Manhattan locations that resemble locations and aesthetics associated with A-Town's earlier version.

Chinatown and other New York City backdrops have become a particular point of discussion among people comparing the two releases.

The Internet Is Divided

The allegations have sparked debate across Instagram, TikTok and other social-media platforms.

Some listeners believe the similarities deserve a closer look, particularly because the songs share the same title and Japanese-themed concept.

Others have questioned whether those similarities are enough to establish plagiarism, noting that common themes, titles and musical styles do not necessarily prove that one artist copied another.

At this point, the online discussion remains an allegation and has not been established as a fact.

A-Town's Version Came First

A-Town's “Konnichiwa” was released as a single on July 5, 2024, and has also been performed live, including during the Yo Who Dis?! performance.

Future's version arrived in 2026 as part of The Real Me, bringing renewed attention to A-Town's earlier recording.

The timing has fueled questions from fans about whether Future or anyone involved with his record was familiar with A-Town's song before creating the newer version.

Future's Camp Has Not Responded

Neither Future nor producers ATL Jacob and Wheezy have publicly addressed the allegations at the time of publication.

Without a public response or additional evidence establishing a connection between the two recordings, it remains unclear whether the similarities are coincidental, the result of shared influences or something more deliberate.

For now, the controversy continues to unfold online, with listeners comparing the two versions and debating whether Future's “Konnichiwa” crossed the line from inspiration into imitation.

A-Town's Philadelphia roots have also become part of the conversation, putting a local spotlight on the comedian and internet personality as his 2024 song receives renewed attention.
