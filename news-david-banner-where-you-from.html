David Banner Connects Southern Rap Generations on “WHERE YOU FROM?”

Music | Hip-Hop

Published: Tuesday, August 18, 2026

David Banner is bringing together some major names from Southern hip-hop with his latest release, “WHERE YOU FROM?”

The new track features Atlanta legend T.I., Memphis veteran Juicy J, and rising Memphis standout Duke Deuce, creating a collaboration that connects different eras and cities within Southern rap.

Representing the South

The title says it all. “WHERE YOU FROM?” puts regional identity at the center of the record, with each artist bringing his own style and Southern influence to the collaboration.

Banner, who has long represented Mississippi in hip-hop, joins forces with artists connected to Atlanta and Memphis. The combination gives the song a strong Southern identity while bringing together artists who represent different chapters of the culture.

Juicy J adds the legacy of Memphis’ legendary Three 6 Mafia sound, while Duke Deuce brings a younger Memphis perspective. T.I. adds his signature Atlanta presence, making the track feel like a meeting of Southern hip-hop generations.

Banner Keeps the Momentum Going

The new song continues David Banner’s work as both an artist and producer. Known for his Southern sound and socially conscious approach, Banner has remained active in hip-hop while continuing to collaborate with artists across the genre.

“WHERE YOU FROM?” gives longtime fans a familiar Southern energy while introducing a lineup that spans multiple generations.

A Track Built for Southern Hip-Hop Fans

With Banner, T.I., Juicy J and Duke Deuce sharing the same record, the song brings together Mississippi, Atlanta and Memphis in one collaboration.

Whether you’re a longtime fan of Southern hip-hop or discovering these artists through the newer generation, “WHERE YOU FROM?” delivers a strong reminder of how influential the South continues to be in rap music.
