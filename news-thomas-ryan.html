New York Man Charged After Deadly House Fire Leads to Homicide Investigation

National News | New York

Published: Sunday, August 9, 2026

A house fire in Orange County has turned into a homicide investigation after authorities discovered a man's body inside the residence, leading to the arrest of his 32-year-old son.

New York State Police identified the suspect as Thomas David Ryan, 32. Investigators had been searching for Ryan after evidence connected him to the death of his father, 70-year-old Robert Ryan, and a fire at the older man's Greenville home.

The investigation began Thursday when emergency crews responded to a reported fire on Greenville Turnpike in Greenville, New York. After firefighters brought the blaze under control, investigators located Robert Ryan deceased inside the residence.

Authorities later determined that the fire appeared to have been intentionally started. That discovery shifted the investigation from a fire response to a suspected homicide.

Police launched a search for Thomas Ryan and warned the public that he could be dangerous. Investigators said Ryan had not been living with his father and was not known to have an established residence in the Greenville area.

According to state police, Ryan had recently lived a transient lifestyle and had spent time at different shelters throughout New York. Investigators followed up on possible locations and tips as they attempted to determine where he had gone after the incident.

Authorities also conducted an extensive review of surveillance footage around the crime scene. Video obtained by ABC News reportedly showed a person believed to be Ryan leaving the area of the burning residence.

The investigation brought an increased police presence to Greenville and nearby communities while officers worked to locate the suspect.

For Robert Ryan's family, the investigation has brought the loss of a loved one into the spotlight. His daughter, Stephanie Coiro, described her father as someone who was deeply respected by his family and the communities he was part of.

She told ABC News that her father was the type of person who welcomed others, helped people in need and made a positive impact on those around him.

Authorities have now taken Ryan into custody, bringing an end to the immediate manhunt. Investigators are continuing to examine the evidence surrounding the death and fire as the case moves forward through the legal system.

The investigation remains active, and additional details may be released by authorities as the case progresses.
