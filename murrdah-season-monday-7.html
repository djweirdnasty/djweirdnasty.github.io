🎙 Murrdah Season Monday Celebrates 3 Years With a Packed Anniversary Episode

This Week on MURRDAH SEASON MONDAY

This week’s Murrdah Season Monday was a special one as the show celebrated its 3rd-year anniversary with a packed episode featuring comedy, music, dancing, and plenty of Philly energy.

Comedian Cloud 9 stepped in as this week’s host, helping guide viewers through an anniversary episode filled with familiar faces and special guests.

Ron Murrdah took time to talk about Philly Phruit Juice, highlighting the brand’s journey and its 9 years of activity in the community.

Princess made her return to the show and spoke about her role as co-captain of the 215 Steppas line dance team, sharing more about the team and her involvement with the dance community.

DJWEIRDNASTY also joined the conversation, reflecting on his 15 years as a DJ and explaining how the name DJWEIRDNASTY was created. He also recapped the recent Jersey Jam, featuring BBW BUNNIES & DON’s and BADLANDZ HAZ, giving viewers a look back at the event and the energy surrounding it.

Ro-Digga brought her usual unfiltered personality to the episode, including a candid conversation about what she says she does to men in the bedroom.

The episode also featured live performances from Showtime Dom and Sista Keilana, adding music and performance energy to an already packed anniversary show.

From celebrating three years of Murrdah Season Monday to highlighting Philly businesses, dance culture, DJs, artists, and live entertainment, this week’s episode brought together a little bit of everything that makes the show unique.

Happy 3rd Anniversary to Murrdah Season Monday! 🎉🔥
