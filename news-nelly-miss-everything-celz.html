Nelly Miss Everything and Everything Celz Keep Philadelphia Laughing

Entertainment

Published: Wednesday, August 19, 2026

Philadelphia’s comedy and entertainment scene is getting another dose of energy from local personalities Nelly Miss Everything, also known as Nelly Roberts, and Everything Celz. The entertainers have been building recognition through comedy performances, media appearances, podcasts, radio, and community events.

The pair has appeared on FOX 29 Philadelphia, bringing attention to their entertainment projects and events. Their television exposure has included discussion and promotion surrounding the Nelly Miss Everything Comedy Bash, while Nelly has also used media appearances to showcase her fitness and wellness brand, Nelly Yoga Fitness.

Nelly Roberts has developed a career that extends well beyond the comedy stage. She is recognized by the nicknames “Yoga Soldier” and “Yoga Brat” and has been involved in podcasting through Think Outside the Box. She is also associated with the Respectfully Speaking radio show on WWD 860 AM.

Meanwhile, Everything Celz continues to be active in Philadelphia-area entertainment, including hosting and performing at comedy events. The two have frequently crossed paths on stages throughout Philadelphia and New Jersey, giving audiences opportunities to see their chemistry in front of a live crowd.

Upcoming Shows

Their upcoming schedule includes Comedy with a Kink 7 on August 29, 2026. They are also scheduled to appear the following evening, August 30, at Punch Line Philly Comedy Club during the Granville Bazemore & Friends show.

From local television appearances to comedy clubs and independent media, Nelly Miss Everything and Everything Celz are continuing to expand their presence in the region. Their combination of humor, personality, and community involvement has helped them become familiar faces among Philadelphia-area entertainment audiences.

With more performances ahead, the two entertainers are showing that Philly’s comedy scene continues to have plenty of homegrown talent ready to take the stage.
