CODEBLUE WORLD Talk Radio on 98.5 Uptown Radio

Wednesday, September 16, 2026 — WJYN 98.5 FM Uptown Radio, Philadelphia, PA

📻🔥 SPINNING ON PHILLY’S WJYN 98.5 FM! 🔥📻

DJWEIRDNASTY was spinning on CODEBLUE WORLD Talk Radio with Elisha Taylor on WJYN 98.5 FM Uptown Radio in Philadelphia!

🎤 Special Guest: Damon from X RUMBLE joined the show for a great conversation.

🚨 And the crew kept pulling up! Ron Murrdah from Glocawear Radio’s Murrdah Season Monday stopped through to promote Code Blue’s flavored Philly Phruit Juice. 🍹🔥

👕 Sturdy MU also came through with an unboxing of the new MARSUS CLOTHING cardigans, showcasing the latest pieces on the show.

It was another night of Philly radio, music, conversation, brands, and culture all coming together on the airwaves! 🎧🔥
