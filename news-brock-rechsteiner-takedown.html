NFL Practice Erupts as Saints Receiver Brock Rechsteiner Brings Wrestling Family Legacy to the Field

Sports | NFL

Published: Tuesday, August 18, 2026

What was supposed to be a routine joint practice between the New Orleans Saints and Dallas Cowboys quickly became one of the more chaotic sessions of training camp.

The two teams reportedly had several heated exchanges Tuesday, but one particular confrontation stood out. Saints wide receiver Brock Rechsteiner took Cowboys safety Jalen Thompson to the ground with a wrestling-style maneuver during the chaos.

Video of the altercation spread across social media, with fans immediately recognizing the significance of Rechsteiner’s last name.

From the Football Field to the Wrestling Ring

Rechsteiner isn’t just another NFL player with a famous family. His father is former professional wrestling star Scott Steiner, who became one of the sport’s most recognizable power wrestlers alongside his brother, Rick Steiner.

The Steiner Brothers built their reputation on physicality and explosive amateur-wrestling-inspired moves, making Brock’s takedown of Thompson an especially ironic moment.

Brock also has another connection to the wrestling world. His cousin is Bron Breakker, the WWE performer whose real name is Bronson Rechsteiner.

So when Brock put Thompson on the ground Tuesday, wrestling fans quickly connected the moment to his family’s history.

How the Altercation Escalated

The confrontation reportedly started with Rechsteiner and Cowboys cornerback Caelen Carson exchanging words and shoves. The situation intensified when Carson grabbed Rechsteiner’s facemask.

Players from both sides rushed toward the developing confrontation, turning the exchange into a larger scrum.

Thompson eventually became involved with Rechsteiner. During the struggle, the Saints receiver managed to get behind the Cowboys safety, secured control around his midsection and brought him down.

The takedown immediately became one of the most recognizable moments from the practice.

More Than One Fight

The incident involving Rechsteiner and Thompson wasn’t an isolated event.

Reports indicated that the Saints and Cowboys had several separate altercations throughout the session, with the number of fights reportedly reaching approximately seven.

Saints center Erik McCoy was also involved in one of the more unusual moments after a Cowboys helmet was thrown during the disorder.

Joint practices are intended to provide teams with competitive preparation against an opponent they don’t normally face during camp. However, the increased physicality can sometimes lead to tempers boiling over.

Rechsteiner’s Complicated Start to His NFL Career

The viral practice incident comes at an unusual point in Rechsteiner’s young professional football career.

The Saints receiver is scheduled to serve a six-game suspension at the beginning of the 2026 regular season after violating the NFL-NFLPA policy concerning performance-enhancing substances.

Despite that setback, Rechsteiner remains a player to watch as he attempts to establish himself in the NFL.

For one afternoon, however, his football career took a backseat to his family’s wrestling legacy.

With the Saints and Cowboys scheduled to meet in a preseason matchup on August 28, Tuesday’s practice could add another layer of intrigue to their upcoming meeting.

One thing is already certain: Brock Rechsteiner has found a way to make his NFL name recognizable well beyond the football field.
