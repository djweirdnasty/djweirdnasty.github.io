Hobbs Names Former Mesa Republican Mayor John Giles as Running Mate

National | Politics

Published: Tuesday, August 11, 2026

Arizona Gov. Katie Hobbs is bringing a former Republican into her campaign for another term, selecting former Mesa Mayor John Giles as her choice for lieutenant governor.

Hobbs announced the decision Tuesday as she prepares for a closely watched November election. Giles served as Mesa’s mayor from 2014 through 2025 and spent most of his political career as a Republican before becoming an independent.

The pair announced their partnership in a video, with Hobbs presenting Giles as someone who can help put Arizona issues ahead of political divisions.

Hobbs pointed to her administration’s record on issues including taxes, border security and public safety as she makes her case for another term.

Giles said his decision to leave the Republican Party came after years of frustration with the direction of the party. He officially became an independent in May and later participated in Arizona’s Democratic primary.

His political shift has been increasingly visible. During the 2024 presidential campaign, Giles backed Kamala Harris and later addressed the Democratic National Convention in Chicago. He criticized the Republican Party’s growing ties to political extremism and said the party no longer represented the political tradition he once supported.

The selection gives Hobbs a running mate with a history in one of Arizona’s largest cities while also signaling an effort to appeal to independent and moderate voters.

Arizona is preparing to elect a lieutenant governor for the first time this year. Voters approved the creation of the position through a ballot measure in 2022.

Hobbs’ Republican opponent will be U.S. Rep. Andy Biggs, a longtime conservative figure who previously served as chairman of the House Freedom Caucus. Biggs won the Republican primary last month after defeating fellow Rep. David Schweikert and business candidates Scott Neely and Ken Miceli.

Biggs has selected former Arizona lawmaker Sine Kerr to join his ticket.

The gubernatorial contest is expected to receive significant national attention because Arizona remains a major battleground. Donald Trump carried the state in the 2016 and 2024 presidential elections, while Joe Biden won Arizona in 2020.

The Cook Political Report currently considers the governor’s race to lean toward the Democratic candidate.

Hobbs narrowly won her first gubernatorial election in 2022, defeating Republican Kari Lake by less than a percentage point. Prior to becoming governor, Hobbs served as secretary of state and held leadership positions in the Arizona Legislature.

Her campaign has highlighted her record of working with Republicans and independents, including more than 900 bipartisan measures she says she has signed into law. She has also promoted efforts involving housing affordability, border security and the repeal of Arizona’s 1864 abortion law.

Hobbs has spent recent months building support beyond the Democratic Party, forming a coalition that includes more than 50 Republican and independent officials and business leaders.

Biggs is taking a different approach, emphasizing his conservative record, relationship with Trump, tax-cut proposals and border-security policies.

With Giles now on Hobbs’ ticket, the governor’s campaign is making a clear appeal to voters who may be dissatisfied with traditional party politics as both sides prepare for a competitive general election.
