Philadelphia Father Killed After Being Caught in Woodland Avenue Gunfire

National | Crime

Published: Wednesday, August 13, 2026

A Philadelphia family is grieving the loss of a 42-year-old father who police say was killed after being caught in gunfire in Southwest Philadelphia.

Emmanuel Oakman was shot multiple times around 7 a.m. Sunday on the 6200 block of Woodland Avenue. Authorities said he later died from his injuries.

An Innocent Bystander

According to police, Oakman was not the intended target. Investigators believe he was an innocent bystander who became caught in the middle of the shooting.

His daughter, Asia Clark, had recently returned to Philadelphia after attending college in Ohio. She is now preparing to say goodbye to her father.

Clark described Oakman as a devoted father who was loved by his family and friends. He leaves behind his daughter and his 18-year-old son, Zaire.

Family members told Clark that Oakman was caught in the crossfire. She believes the location of his injuries indicates that he may have been attempting to get away from the gunfire.

“He was a great dad,” Clark said while remembering her father.

Family Remembrances

Oakman was also remembered as someone with a good sense of humor who was willing to lend a hand when someone needed help.

For his family, the tragedy has left them trying to make sense of a life lost in a moment of violence.

Clark said she hopes the person responsible is eventually identified and taken off the streets before another family experiences a similar loss.

Investigation Ongoing

Philadelphia police continue to investigate the shooting and are asking anyone who may have information to come forward.

Anyone with information can contact or text Philadelphia police at 215-686-TIPS (8477). Tips can be provided anonymously.

This story is based on information reported by police and local news reports. The investigation remains ongoing, and no suspect has been identified in connection with the shooting.
