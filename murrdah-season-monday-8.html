Murrdah Season Monday Episode 8: Nuclearman, Fab Morvan & Code Blue

This week on Murrdah Season Monday, Elisha Taylor and Ayo Weird go back and forth while trying to promote Code Blue’s World Radio Show on 98.5 FM WJYN Philly Uptown Radio.

Special guest Nuclearman stops by to talk about receiving a permit to perform in Atlantic City and shares his experience dealing with a “Karen.”

Later, Fab Morvan of Milli Vanilli joins the conversation to reflect on his history in the music business and discuss how the industry has changed throughout the years.
