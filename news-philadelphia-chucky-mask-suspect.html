Masked Suspect Sought After Allegedly Threatening People in Center City Philadelphia

National | Crime

Published: Tuesday, August 18, 2026

Philadelphia police are searching for a young man accused of frightening and confronting several people in Center City while wearing a mask resembling the horror character Chucky.

Investigators say the encounters happened during the early morning hours of Wednesday, August 12, over a period of approximately 20 minutes. Police believe the suspect approached multiple pedestrians before focusing on a woman who was jogging near Philadelphia City Hall.

Jogger Followed and Threatened

According to investigators, the man followed the jogger while holding a cellphone and allegedly asked her whether she was "ready to die." The woman reportedly ran away while calling for help and suffered an injury to her leg during the incident.

Police believe the jogger may not have been the only person targeted. Authorities say they have identified at least six people who may have been approached or intimidated by the masked individual, although investigators believe additional victims could still come forward.

Witness Accounts

One witness described seeing the suspect near a La Colombe coffee shop before he approached the jogger. The witness said she attempted to warn the runner after noticing the masked man following her.

The witness later confronted the suspect herself. She told investigators that she screamed and kicked him after he approached her. She said the man's behavior appeared unusual and that he did not seem to have a clear objective.

Another person working near the coffee shop reportedly encountered the suspect before a security guard intervened.

Possible Recording, Suspect Description

Police say the suspect was also seen using his cellphone during some of the encounters, leading investigators to believe he may have been recording the incidents. Detectives are reviewing social media in hopes of finding footage that could help identify him.

Authorities describe the suspect as a Black male with a thin build, approximately 5 feet 8 inches tall and believed to be between 16 and 20 years old. Police say that at one point he appeared to suggest that he had a weapon, although investigators have not reported recovering or seeing an actual weapon.

Investigation Ongoing

The incidents have prompted concerns among people who work and exercise in Center City during the early morning hours.

Police say the suspect could face charges including terroristic threats, harassment, simple assault, reckless endangerment and intimidation if he is identified and arrested.

Anyone who believes they encountered the masked individual or has information that could help investigators is being asked to contact Philadelphia police at 215-686-TIPS (8477). Authorities are particularly interested in hearing from anyone who may have been approached by the suspect but has not yet reported the encounter.

This story is based on information reported by police. The investigation remains ongoing, and no suspect has been identified or arrested in connection with these incidents.
