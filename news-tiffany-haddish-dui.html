Tiffany Haddish Resolves Georgia DUI Case With Guilty Plea

Entertainment | Celebrity

Published: Tuesday, August 12, 2026

Actress and comedian Tiffany Haddish has reached a deal in a long-running DUI case in Fayette County, Georgia, entering a guilty plea that eliminates the need for a scheduled trial.

Haddish pleaded guilty to DUI driving less safe in connection with her 2022 arrest in Peachtree City. The remaining traffic-related charges were dismissed as part of the agreement.

Haddish appeared remotely during the court proceeding and will not serve additional jail time. The court credited her with time already served following her arrest.

Under the agreement, Haddish's Georgia driver's license will be suspended. She will also serve 12 months of probation, avoid drugs and alcohol, undergo testing and complete a DUI education course.

The case dates back to January 2022, when police responded to a 911 report about a driver who appeared to be asleep behind the wheel near an intersection. Officers later found Haddish's vehicle near a residential property and reported that she showed signs of impairment before being arrested.

The case remained unresolved for more than four years as both sides filed motions and requested delays. Haddish's attorneys recently argued that the lengthy proceedings violated her constitutional right to a speedy trial. A judge rejected that request, finding that some of the delay was connected to requests made by the defense.

With the negotiated plea, the case has now been closed without a full trial.
