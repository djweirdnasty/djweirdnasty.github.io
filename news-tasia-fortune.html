Investigation Underway After Mississippi Woman Is Found Dead Hanging From Tree

National News | Mississippi

Published: Monday, August 10, 2026

JACKSON, Miss. — Authorities are investigating the death of a 29-year-old woman whose body was discovered hanging from a tree behind a vacant residence in Jackson earlier this week.

Tasia Fortune was found on Monday, Aug. 3, according to reports from the Mississippi Free Press, The Clarion Ledger and ABC affiliate WAPT. Authorities believe her body may have remained at the location for several days before it was discovered.

Officials have not yet released a cause or manner of death. Hinds County Coroner Jeramiah Howard told The Clarion Ledger that Fortune's remains were transported to the Mississippi State Medical Examiner's Office for an autopsy.

The Jackson Police Department confirmed that officers are conducting a death investigation. In a statement issued Monday, police said crime scene investigators were working to establish what happened and determine the circumstances surrounding Fortune's death.

At this time, authorities have not publicly indicated whether they suspect suicide, homicide or another cause.

The circumstances surrounding the discovery have prompted questions from members of the Jackson community and renewed attention to Mississippi's history of racial violence, including the state's documented history of lynchings.

Those concerns have also led some community members and officials to call for a thorough examination of the case. Jackson City Councilman Kenneth Stokes reportedly contacted the U.S. Attorney's Office seeking assistance with the investigation.

"Surely, somebody should say something," Stokes said, according to WAPT.

The case has drawn additional scrutiny because of previous incidents in Mississippi involving Black residents whose deaths were initially classified as suicides. In some cases, relatives later challenged those determinations and sought additional investigations.

For now, officials say the investigation remains active, with the pending autopsy expected to provide important information about how Fortune died.

The Hinds County Coroner's Office, Mississippi State Medical Examiner's Office and Jackson Police Department had not provided additional information when contacted for comment.

A GoFundMe campaign established to assist Fortune's family with funeral expenses describes her as a "strong-minded, big-hearted, vibrant young woman." The fundraiser says her death has left her family and friends devastated as they prepare to lay her to rest.

The fundraiser also states that Fortune's loved ones are seeking justice and remain hopeful that the circumstances surrounding her death will eventually be fully understood.

As investigators await the autopsy results, questions remain about what happened to Fortune before her body was discovered and whether anyone else may have been involved.

No determination regarding the cause or manner of Fortune's death has been publicly announced.
