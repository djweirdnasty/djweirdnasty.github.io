$15 Nail Repair Dispute Turns Into Gunfire at Phoenix Salon, Police Say

National | Crime

Published: Tuesday, September 1, 2026

A dispute over a $15 nail repair allegedly escalated into gunfire at a Phoenix salon, sending nearly two dozen people scrambling for safety, authorities said.

Essence Mire, 26, is accused of shooting into Element Nail Bar on Aug. 30 after an argument with an employee over the price of repairing a broken fingernail, according to court documents cited by local news outlets.

Investigators say the confrontation became physical before Mire left the business. Police allege she punched an employee and then returned carrying a firearm.

Authorities say Mire fired into the salon, striking the front glass door and shattering it. At the time, approximately 18 people were reportedly inside the business.

Customers and employees reacted by running from the salon through the front and rear exits or taking cover inside, according to investigators. No one was reported injured in the incident.

Police later located Mire at a home in the Phoenix area and arrested her.

According to court documents, investigators allege Mire acknowledged shooting at the business because she wanted to frighten the employees into providing the $15 nail repair at no cost.

Mire faces multiple charges, including aggravated assault, assault, discharging a firearm at a non-residential property and discharging a firearm within city limits. Her bond was reportedly set at $100,000.

The allegations against Mire have not been proven in court. The case remains pending.
