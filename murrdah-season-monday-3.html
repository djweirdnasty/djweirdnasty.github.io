🎙️ Murrdah Season Monday Karmelo Anthony Trial, Fox29 Feature, SOL Sounds & Flavor Team Earrings

This Week on MURRDAH SEASON MONDAY

Princess opened this week’s Trending Topics segment, immediately setting the tone for a lively discussion that centered on the highly publicized Karmelo Anthony trial.

The conversation quickly turned into a heated debate between Code Blue, Ayo Weird and Thyeosthyco, with each bringing a different perspective to the case. The three discussed the circumstances surrounding the trial, examined different aspects of the case and shared their opinions about what they believe is really happening.

The debate gave listeners an opportunity to hear several contrasting viewpoints as the conversation moved back and forth between the participants.

Following the Trending Topics discussion, special guest performer Thyeosthyco joined Everything Celz and Miss Everything Nelly for a conversation covering their latest developments.

Everything Celz and Miss Everything Nelly discussed their upcoming Fox29 feature on August 29, giving listeners a preview of the appearance and the attention surrounding the opportunity. The Fox29 feature specifically highlights Everything Celz and Miss Everything Nelly.

The group also announced a brand-new podcast that will air immediately following Murrdah Season Monday on Glocawear Radio. The new program is expected to give listeners another opportunity to hear from the personalities and guests connected to the station.

The show then moved into its weekly trivia competition, where Code Blue emerged as this week’s winner. After answering the trivia question correctly, Code Blue took home the Cutwater promo and shared information about the upcoming Ghetto House Party, scheduled for Saturday, August 29.

The spotlight then shifted to Ayo Weird, who announced the launch of SOL: Sounds of Logan, a DJ booking software platform created to help connect DJs with clients and simplify the booking process.

Ayo Weird revealed that SOL: Sounds of Logan is now available through djweirdnasty.com, marking another expansion of his work in the DJ and technology space.

Ron Murrdah closed out the broadcast by promoting the latest Flavor Team earrings, with the new accessories being presented as a special option for the ladies.

With a heated trial discussion, a special guest performance, a Fox29 feature, a new podcast announcement, trivia, an upcoming party and the launch of new DJ booking software, the latest Glocawear Radio broadcast delivered a full lineup of entertainment, conversation and announcements.
