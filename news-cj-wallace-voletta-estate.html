CJ Wallace Takes Legal Action Over Changes to Voletta Wallace’s Estate

Music News

Published: Friday, August 21, 2026

A new legal dispute is unfolding around the estate of Voletta Wallace, the mother of late hip-hop icon The Notorious B.I.G.

Christopher “CJ” Wallace Jr., Biggie’s son, has reportedly gone to court over changes made to his grandmother’s estate plan shortly before her death in February 2025.

The case centers on an amendment to Voletta Wallace’s will that was reportedly signed just over two weeks before she died.

Grandchildren Were Listed Under Earlier Estate Plan

According to reports, Voletta Wallace’s 2020 estate plan included her grandchildren among the people expected to receive personal belongings and other assets.

Those assets reportedly included some of Biggie’s music awards, jewelry, fur pieces, a Mercedes-Benz SUV and property in Jamaica.

CJ Wallace claims the later amendment substantially changed those arrangements and altered who would have responsibilities connected to the estate.

The filing reportedly identifies Wayne Barrow, a former manager associated with Biggie, as a major figure in the revised estate arrangement.

Questions Raised About the Amendment

CJ is challenging the validity of the document and has raised concerns about the circumstances surrounding its signing.

His filing reportedly points to Voletta Wallace’s health during the final period of her life, including her use of medication and her limited physical condition.

He also questions the signatures appearing on the amendment, describing them as noticeably different from previous signatures. His legal action asks the court to investigate the signatures and determine whether the document should remain valid.

CJ is also seeking Barrow’s removal from his role in managing or overseeing portions of the estate.

Biggie’s Catalog Adds Another Layer

The dispute arrives during a larger series of legal and financial disagreements involving Biggie’s estate.

Voletta Wallace had maintained an interest connected to her son’s musical assets following his death in 1997. A portion of that interest was later involved in a transaction with Primary Wave, a major music publishing and rights company.

Barrow has also been involved in litigation concerning money connected to that deal. A recent court decision reportedly allowed him to receive a portion of distributions associated with Voletta Wallace’s interest.

That development has added another layer to the disagreement over who should control and benefit from assets tied to Biggie’s legacy.

The Case Is Still Unresolved

CJ Wallace’s filing does not establish that the disputed amendment was forged or legally invalid. Those issues will ultimately be determined through the legal process.

For now, the case raises questions about the circumstances surrounding the final changes to Voletta Wallace’s estate plan and who should have authority over the assets connected to her family.

The dispute could have significant implications for the Wallace family and the continued management of property and interests associated with one of hip-hop’s most influential artists.
