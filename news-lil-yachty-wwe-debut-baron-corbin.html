Lil Yachty’s WWE Debut Draws Fan Backlash as Rapper Prepares for Five-Minute Match With Baron Corbin

Music | WWE

Published: Sunday, August 23, 2026

Lil Yachty is preparing to make his WWE in-ring debut, but the rapper’s upcoming match against Baron Corbin has already become a lightning rod for criticism among wrestling fans.

WWE announced Friday that Yachty will face Corbin at Sunday Night’s Main Event on Sept. 6 in Atlanta, putting the Grammy-nominated rapper in the middle of an ongoing storyline involving Corbin and former United States Champion Trick Williams.

The unusual matchup has generated a divided reaction. While some fans have questioned WWE’s decision to give Yachty a singles match against an established wrestler, reports indicate that the rapper has been taking the opportunity seriously and has spent months preparing for his first official WWE bout.

Yachty Has Reportedly Been Training for Months

According to reports cited by wrestling media, Yachty has been working out at the WWE Performance Center in Orlando, Florida, as he prepares for his debut.

Bryan Alvarez reported that the rapper has worked with WWE talent and trainers, including Ivy Nile and Ari Levy. Another report from False Finish said Yachty has earned the respect of people within WWE and the broader wrestling industry because of his commitment to training.

The reports also indicate that Yachty could remain involved with WWE programming beyond his Atlanta match, suggesting the appearance may be more than a one-night celebrity cameo.

That level of preparation could prove important because WWE has increasingly positioned celebrity appearances as legitimate parts of its storylines rather than simply promotional appearances.

The Match Has Major Consequences for Trick Williams

Although Yachty is the one stepping into the ring, the match is directly tied to Trick Williams’ pursuit of the United States Championship.

Williams lost the title to Corbin at SummerSlam after becoming distracted during the match when Corbin put Yachty through a table.

Since then, Williams has been seeking another opportunity to challenge for the championship.

During Friday’s episode of SmackDown, tensions escalated when Yachty confronted Corbin and used Williams’ signature “Trick Stick” against him.

Corbin responded by agreeing to give Williams a potential championship rematch—but only if Williams accepted a unique condition.

Corbin will face Yachty at Sunday Night’s Main Event. If Yachty survives five minutes without being defeated, Williams earns another opportunity at the United States Championship.

If Corbin manages to defeat Yachty before the five-minute mark, Williams loses his chance at another title match.

The stipulation gives the celebrity match an immediate storyline consequence while allowing WWE to keep the focus on the ongoing Corbin-Williams rivalry.

Fans Have Criticized Triple H Over the Match

The announcement has nevertheless sparked considerable criticism online, with some wrestling fans directing their frustration toward WWE Chief Content Officer Triple H, whose real name is Paul Levesque.

Several fans questioned why WWE would book Yachty in a singles match while other wrestlers have struggled to receive prominent opportunities.

Some social-media reactions accused Triple H of having “lost the plot,” while others called for changes to WWE’s creative direction.

The backlash reflects a broader debate within professional wrestling over celebrity involvement. WWE has a long history of bringing musicians, actors and athletes into its programming, but fans have often disagreed over whether those appearances strengthen storylines or take opportunities away from full-time performers.

Yachty’s match will ultimately be judged by how well he performs and whether WWE can make the storyline feel meaningful rather than simply using the rapper as a novelty attraction.

Atlanta Could Give Yachty a Home-Crowd Advantage

The location could work in Yachty’s favor.

The Sept. 6 event is scheduled for Atlanta, Yachty’s hometown. That means the rapper is likely to receive a significantly warmer reaction from the crowd than he might have received in another WWE market.

His familiarity with the city could also allow WWE to emphasize his connection to Atlanta as part of the presentation surrounding his debut.

Celebrity wrestlers have occasionally surprised skeptical audiences by demonstrating that they understand the physical demands and performance aspects of professional wrestling. Yachty now has an opportunity to do the same.

WWE Moving Forward Despite the Backlash

Despite the negative reaction from portions of the fan base, there is no indication that WWE plans to cancel or substantially change the match.

Instead, the company appears committed to using Yachty’s involvement to advance the Corbin-Williams feud while giving the rapper an opportunity to establish himself inside the ring.

The five-minute stipulation also gives WWE a relatively simple way to protect Yachty if the match is designed primarily to advance the larger storyline. He does not necessarily have to defeat Corbin; simply surviving the time limit would accomplish Williams’ objective.

That leaves several possibilities for the Atlanta event, including Yachty surviving the full five minutes, Corbin defeating him quickly, or an outside interruption changing the outcome.

For now, the biggest question is whether Yachty can turn months of reported training into a performance that wins over skeptical wrestling fans.

His WWE debut is scheduled for Sept. 6 at Sunday Night’s Main Event in Atlanta, where his five-minute battle with Corbin could determine whether Trick Williams gets another shot at the United States Championship.
