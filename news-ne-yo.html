Ne-Yo Hit With Lawsuit Over Alleged Dog Attack at Georgia Property

Music News

Published: Tuesday, August 11, 2026

Grammy-winning singer Ne-Yo is being sued by a woman who says she was injured after one of the entertainer’s dogs allegedly attacked her while she was making a delivery.

Dominique Bridgett filed the lawsuit in Fulton County, Georgia, over an incident she says occurred in July 2025 at Ne-Yo’s residence in Alpharetta.

Bridgett alleges that she was delivering a package when the dog suddenly came toward her and bit her leg. She claims the animal attacked without any warning or action on her part that would have provoked it.

The lawsuit names Ne-Yo, whose legal name is Shaffer Chimere Smith, and accuses him of failing to properly secure or control the animal.

Bridgett further alleges that Smith knew or should have known the dog could behave aggressively. She claims that proper precautions should have been taken to prevent the animal from coming into contact with people visiting the property.

The woman says the alleged attack left her dealing with physical injuries as well as emotional distress. According to the complaint, she has accumulated $9,039.50 in medical expenses so far.

She is also seeking compensation for additional damages, including future medical consequences, physical and emotional suffering and the alleged loss of her ability to enjoy everyday activities as she did before the incident.

Bridgett has requested that the dispute be decided by a jury.

Ne-Yo’s representatives had not provided a public response to the allegations when the lawsuit was reported.

The case is another example of legal disputes involving alleged dog attacks at celebrity residences.

Earlier, singer Chris Brown faced a lawsuit stemming from a 2020 incident involving a housekeeper at his California home. Maria Avila alleged that she suffered devastating injuries during the attack.

Avila’s case eventually went before a jury. During the trial, she described lasting injuries to her arm and face, significant scarring and psychological trauma. Jurors ultimately awarded her $13 million in damages in June.

The allegations against Ne-Yo are part of an ongoing civil lawsuit and have not been established as fact in court. No determination of liability has been made against the singer.
