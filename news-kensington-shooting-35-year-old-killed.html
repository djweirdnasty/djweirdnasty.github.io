35-Year-Old Man Killed in Early-Morning Kensington Shooting as Philadelphia Police Search for Suspect

National | Crime

Published: Sunday, August 23, 2026

A 35-year-old man was fatally shot early Sunday morning in Philadelphia’s Kensington neighborhood, prompting a homicide investigation as authorities work to determine what led to the deadly gunfire.

Philadelphia police said officers were dispatched to the 3200 block of Kensington Avenue shortly after 5:15 a.m. Sunday, Aug. 23, following a report of a person with a gun. When officers arrived, they found a man who was unresponsive and suffering from several gunshot wounds.

According to police, the victim had been shot in the back, chest and right leg.

Emergency personnel transported him to Temple University Hospital, but doctors were unable to save him. Police said he was pronounced dead at approximately 5:36 a.m., only minutes after officers initially responded to the scene.

Authorities have not publicly identified the victim pending notification of his family.

Police Continue Search for Suspect

Investigators have not announced an arrest, and police have not publicly identified a suspect or released a possible motive in the shooting.

The circumstances surrounding the gunfire remain under investigation. Detectives are expected to examine the scene, review any available surveillance footage and interview witnesses who may have been in the area around the time of the shooting.

The early-morning timing of the incident could make witnesses and surveillance footage particularly important as investigators attempt to reconstruct what happened before and after the shooting.

As of Sunday afternoon, authorities had not announced whether the victim and shooter knew one another or whether the shooting was connected to any other incident.

Kensington Shooting Adds to Homicide Investigation

The fatal shooting occurred in Kensington, a Philadelphia neighborhood that has continued to face persistent concerns involving gun violence and other public-safety issues.

Police have not indicated that the shooting was connected to a broader series of incidents. Officials also have not released information suggesting that there is an ongoing threat to the public.

The investigation remains active, and detectives are asking anyone who witnessed the shooting or has information about the circumstances leading up to it to come forward.

Philadelphia police are offering a $20,000 reward for information that leads to an arrest and conviction in the homicide.

Anyone with information can contact the Philadelphia Police Department’s tip line at 215-686-TIPS (8477). Police said information can be provided anonymously.

The investigation remains ongoing, and additional details—including the victim’s identity, a possible motive and information about a suspect—could be released as detectives continue their work.
