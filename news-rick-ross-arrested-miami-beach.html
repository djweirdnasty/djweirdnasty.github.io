Rick Ross Arrested in Miami Beach Domestic Violence Case

Music News

Published: Thursday, October 1, 2026

MIAMI BEACH, Fla. — Rapper Rick Ross was taken into custody Thursday following an investigation into allegations of domestic violence involving a woman he was reportedly in a relationship with.

Ross, whose legal name is William Leonard Roberts II, surrendered to Miami Beach police and was booked at the Turner Guilford Knight Correctional Center. Authorities said the case involves an alleged confrontation that took place Aug. 28 at Ross’ Star Island residence.

The 50-year-old artist is facing a felony charge of battery by strangulation and a separate misdemeanor battery charge. The charges are allegations and have not been proven in court.

According to the arrest affidavit, the woman told investigators that she and Ross had been together for approximately a year and were living at the same residence when the alleged incident occurred.

Police said the dispute began after Ross questioned the woman about being tagged in an Instagram photograph. Investigators allege that the argument escalated into physical contact, with the woman reporting that Ross struck her.

The affidavit contains additional allegations involving an incident in a bathroom. The woman told investigators that Ross hit her in the chest and allegedly interfered with her breathing by pulling and twisting the collar of her hoodie. She also alleged that he grabbed her by her hair, pushed her against a wall and struck her in the face, resulting in an injury to her lip.

The woman eventually left the residence and later provided investigators with photographs documenting injuries, according to police. The image above was supplied with the materials for this story and has not been independently verified.

Detectives also reviewed surveillance footage from the property. Investigators said the video showed a friend attempting to pick the woman up from the residence. Police said Ross later posted an image on social media that included the same person.

Ross Denies Allegations

Ross has rejected the accusations and maintains that he did not commit the alleged acts.

In a statement posted to social media Thursday, the rapper described the accusations as fabricated and said he was confident the matter would ultimately be resolved.

His attorneys, Bradley Horenstein and Steve Sadow, have similarly denied the allegations. They said Ross has pleaded not guilty and asked the public to allow the legal process to proceed before reaching conclusions about the case.

Ross appeared remotely during a bond hearing Thursday. Miami-Dade Circuit Judge Mindy Glazer determined that probable cause existed based on the information presented in the arrest report.

The court set Ross’ total bond at $5,000, consisting of $4,000 for the felony charge and $1,000 for the misdemeanor.

The judge also issued a protective order requiring Ross to stay away from the woman involved in the case and prohibiting direct or indirect contact, including communication through other people or social media.

Ross was additionally ordered to surrender firearms and was prohibited from possessing firearms while the case remains pending.

After posting bond, Ross left the correctional facility and did not take questions from reporters. He indicated that he intended to travel internationally for scheduled performances.

Ross’ next major court date is currently scheduled for Oct. 22.

Rick Ross’ Career

The Miami native has been a major figure in hip-hop since releasing his debut album Port of Miami in 2006. He later founded Maybach Music Group and built a career that includes numerous hit records and Grammy nominations.

The criminal allegations against Ross remain unresolved. His arrest and the court’s probable-cause determination do not constitute a finding of guilt, and the case will continue through the judicial process.
