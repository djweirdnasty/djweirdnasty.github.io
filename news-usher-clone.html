Usher Laughs Off Viral Clone Rumors Following New Jersey Concert With Chris Brown

Music News

Published: Monday, August 10, 2026

Usher found himself at the center of an unexpected internet conspiracy following his performance with Chris Brown at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

The two R&B stars performed back-to-back shows on Aug. 7 and 8 as part of their co-headlining Raymond & Brown Tour, but footage from the first night quickly sparked an unusual conversation online: some social-media users began claiming that the man performing onstage wasn't actually Usher.

Instead, posts circulating across TikTok, X and Instagram suggested that the singer had somehow been replaced by a clone.

The speculation gained enough attention that Usher himself appeared to respond in the comments of concert footage shared online.

One comment attributed to the singer read, "Ya'll are funny…clone? They can't clone this??" accompanied by laughing emojis. In another response, Usher appeared to poke fun at the theory, writing, "How ya'll come up with this? AI ain't that advanced."

The comments were shared by an Instagram account dedicated to updates from the Raymond & Brown Tour.

The online speculation appears to have originated from concert videos in which some viewers claimed the performer looked or moved differently from Usher. Some social-media users focused on his appearance, including his facial features and tattoos, while others questioned why he continued wearing sunglasses during portions of the performance.

One TikTok commenter summed up the confusion by writing that they could see Usher but were still unsure whether they were actually watching him.

Another joked that the performer was "Unsure" rather than Usher.

Despite the online theories, there has been no credible evidence that Usher was replaced by a clone, and the claims remain an internet conspiracy rather than an established fact.

The unusual discussion came after a weather delay pushed back the start of Friday night's concert. Once the show got underway, however, the performance delivered the spectacle fans had expected from the two longtime R&B stars.

The three-hour show featured elaborate choreography, acrobatic performances and appearances from several guest artists, including Lil Baby and 50 Cent. Usher and Brown also shared the stage during portions of the concert, giving fans an opportunity to see the two performers collaborate during their joint tour.

While the concert itself was a major attraction, clips from the performance quickly took on a life of their own online.

For Usher, the simplest response appeared to be humor. Rather than treating the bizarre claims seriously, the singer joined the conversation and appeared to laugh along with fans questioning whether the man onstage was really him.

For now, the "clone" theory remains exactly that — a viral social-media rumor with no verified evidence behind it.
