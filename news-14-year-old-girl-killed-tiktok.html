14-Year-Old Girl Killed While Filming TikTok Videos; Teen Charged in Washington, D.C. Shooting

National | Crime

Published: Friday, August 28, 2026

A 14-year-old Washington, D.C., girl was fatally shot while spending time with other teenagers and recording TikTok videos, and authorities now allege that a boy who was with her pulled the trigger.

The suspect, who was 15 when the shooting occurred, has since been charged with second-degree murder while armed in connection with the death of eighth grader Zytina Mason-Brown. Police have not publicly released the teenager’s name because he was a minor at the time of the alleged offense.

The shooting happened May 21, 2025, inside an apartment in the 1700 block of Benning Road in Washington, D.C.

According to investigators, Zytina was with another girl and the teenage suspect when the gunfire occurred. Authorities say she had been recording videos with a firearm that the suspect allegedly took from his uncle.

Initial account described the shooting as accidental

When police arrived at the apartment shortly after 5 p.m., they found Zytina unconscious and suffering from a gunshot wound. She was not breathing and was pronounced dead at the scene.

The teenage boy initially gave investigators a different explanation for what had happened.

Authorities said he claimed Zytina had accidentally shot herself in the head while handling the firearm. The circumstances surrounding the weapon led to the boy being arrested on a gun-possession charge at the time.

The case, however, did not end there.

As investigators continued examining the circumstances of Zytina’s death, the medical examiner ultimately determined that her death was a homicide. That determination was made several months after the shooting, on Aug. 19, 2025.

Police subsequently offered a $25,000 reward for information that could help establish what happened inside the apartment.

Witness later gave investigators a different account

Investigators’ understanding of the case reportedly changed after the other girl who had been present came forward months later with a different account of the shooting.

According to the witness, Zytina had been recording social media videos while handling the gun. The witness said the teenage boy began dancing near Zytina while she was filming.

Zytina allegedly pushed him away before handing him the firearm and sitting down.

The witness told authorities that the boy then sat across from Zytina, pointed the gun at her and fired.

The account directly contradicted the suspect’s earlier claim that Zytina had shot herself.

Investigation continued for more than a year

The shooting prompted investigators to examine more than just the statements given by those inside the apartment.

Authorities reportedly reviewed social media conversations and photographs that appeared to show Zytina after she had been shot. Investigators have also been working to determine whether additional people were present when the gun was fired.

According to the witness account reported by authorities, the suspect’s mother was in the home and came toward the room after hearing the gunshot.

The witness allegedly told police that the boy instructed her not to say anything about what had happened before authorities were contacted.

The case remained under investigation as detectives worked to reconcile the conflicting accounts and determine exactly how Zytina was killed.

Teen now faces murder charge

More than a year after the shooting, police announced that the teenager had been charged with second-degree murder while armed.

He was 15 at the time of the killing and is now 17.

Because of his age, authorities have not publicly identified him.

The allegations against him remain accusations, and the murder charge will have to be resolved through the legal process. The case also highlights the dangers that can arise when young people have access to unsecured firearms, particularly when weapons are being handled or displayed while recording social media content.

For Zytina’s family, the case has brought renewed attention to a death that authorities initially investigated under a very different set of circumstances. Investigators are continuing to pursue evidence as they work to establish a complete account of what happened inside the apartment on the afternoon of May 21, 2025.
